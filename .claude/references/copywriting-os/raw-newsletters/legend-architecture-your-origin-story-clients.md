- Function: Establishes psychological entry point and activates mirror neuron systems

- Function: Amplifies emotional resonance through strategic vulnerability deployment

Here's what my linguistic analysis AI revealed: Internal struggles using anxiety-spectrum words ("worried," "fearful," "uncertain") generated significantly higher engagement than frustration-spectrum words ("angry," "annoyed," "disappointed").

The algorithm doesn't lie. Specific emotional vocabulary categories trigger measurably different neural responses.

Masters Framework Application: Review your prospects' emails and calls to identify their most common emotional language.

If they say "I'm uncertain whether..." then use "uncertain." Match their exact emotional vocabulary, not your interpretation of how they feel.

This is algorithmic empathy engineering.
