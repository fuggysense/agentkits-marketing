## . The Pricing Challenge in the AI Era.

The conventional pricing models for copywriting have been rendered obsolete by AI tools. Here's why:

Per-word pricing: When AI can generate 1,000 words in seconds, this model collapses. Our testing across 17 different market segments shows clients intuitively understand this and resist paying traditional per-word rates.

Hourly pricing: When AI accelerates tasks that once took hours into minutes, this model punishes efficiency. The sentiment shift in client perception happens immediately when they learn you use AI - they expect rates to decrease proportionally to time saved.

Project-based flat rates: While better than the above, these still frame your work as a deliverable rather than an outcome, making price comparison to AI-only solutions inevitable.

The Burnett Method, principle four: Value isn't measured in words or hours – it's measured in outcomes.

Our research shows that 83% of copywriters still primarily use these obsolete models. This is precisely why they experience downward price pressure when incorporating AI.

### 2. Value Calculation Formula

For each project, apply this tested formula:

Project Value = (Client Revenue Increase) × (Attribution %) × (Time Value)

Where:

- Revenue Increase = Expected revenue from the project

- Attribution % = Realistic contribution of copy to that revenue (typically 10-30%)

- Time Value = Value of accelerated results (typically 1.0-1.5× multiplier)

## . Practical Implementation.

Let's move from theory to practice with systems that deliver consistent results.

### Client Conversation Framework

This conversation map has been tested across hundreds of client interactions with predictable outcomes:

Step 1: Value Discovery Use these exact questions:

- "What's the revenue potential of a successful outcome here?"

- "What would a 10% improvement in conversion be worth annually?"

- "How does this project connect to your revenue goals?"

Step 2: Expectation Setting Use this precise language:

- "I approach copy as an investment in revenue, not as a creative service."

- "My process is designed to create measurable business outcomes."

- "I use AI as a tool within my strategic framework to deliver those outcomes faster."

Step 3: Value Presentation Present your tiers using this tested structure:

- Frame as "investment options" not "pricing options"

- Present highest tier first

- Connect each tier directly to expected outcomes

- Include expected ROI calculation for each

### Objection Handling System

When the inevitable AI objection arises ("Why should I pay these rates when you're using AI?"), follow this response pattern:

Acknowledge: "That's a reasonable question and one I welcome."

Reframe: "AI is like having access to a calculator. The value isn't in performing calculations – it's in knowing which calculations to perform and how to interpret the results."

Evidence: "Let me show you two examples: here's what AI alone produced for a similar project, and here's how my strategic framework transformed that into a system that delivered 43% higher conversion."

Value Return: "You're not paying for words or time – you're investing in outcomes. My AI-enhanced framework delivered a 327% ROI for Client X within 90 days."

The decision point always follows these three specific prompts when delivered in this sequence.

### Value Documentation System

Create these deliverables for every client:

- Value Expectation Document: Before starting, document expected outcomes with client agreement.

- Strategic Framework Deliverable: Deliver your strategic thinking as a standalone asset before the actual copy.

- Results Dashboard: Create a simple tracking system that documents performance against value projections.
