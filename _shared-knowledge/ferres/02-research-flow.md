# Ferres Research Flow — his complete pre-ad research process as a runnable stage map
Primary lectures: Part 3 (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads), Part 5 demo (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo), Master Prompt List (text/18), Control Challenge Checklist (text/17). Cross-swept: Statics Playbook (24), Q&A call (09), Tools List (text/19).
Last distilled: 2026-06-10.

## Operating principles
1. **Research is Stage 1 of 6** (research → ideate → script → create → edit → launch); he calls it the phase the ad "will live or die by" — research informs messaging, messaging informs script (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:01:32], [00:02:54]).
2. **Budget rule of thumb:** research is the longest phase of the whole build — "four hours sharpening the axe" out of six (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:02:16]; 06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:16:22]).
3. **Never start from scratch.** Start with the client's proven winners and competitors who have already paid to test; extract, then branch into new angles (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:04:43]).
4. **Messaging beats visuals.** "You will never out-visual a bad hook" — visuals are downstream of research (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:16:35]; 09_bonus-q-a-call-for-first-20-buyers [00:32:39]).
5. **Bar for done:** the ad should "feel like you read their mind" — verbatim customer phrasing, not paraphrase (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:03:29], [00:17:53]).

## What he researches, source by source
- **Client's own best-performing ads first** — identify which existing ads work and why. Use an ad-analytics tool (he names Motion ⚠️DATED) to split metrics: best hook = retention past 3s, best body = click rate among watchers; splice winning hook onto winning body as a starting point (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:05:25], [00:06:49]).
- **Competitor paid ads** via Meta Ads Library ⚠️PLATFORM — read the OLDEST ads first: still running 3–6 months later ≈ proven profitable (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:09:24], [00:10:04]). Only model competitors spending heavily, not dabblers (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:13:00]). Find competitors via: client interview, YouTube "people also watch," Instagram suggested-follows (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:10:18]).
- **Competitor funnels end-to-end** — click the ad, walk the funnel, note application questions and follow-up cadence; capture sales pages with GoFullPage ⚠️DATED, screen-record VSLs in Loom at 2x for auto-transcripts (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:11:12], [00:12:47], [00:13:06]).
- **Organic winners** (client's + competitors') — rank any account's reels by views/comments with a sort extension (he uses SortFeed ⚠️DATED); take top 5 competitors × their 10 best niche-relevant videos, feed the transcripts to AI and ask "what do these all have in common?" (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:13:49], [00:15:06], [00:16:04]).
- **Comment sections** — free objection research in the customer's own words (scam / too hard / too expensive). Save the comments, then reuse the *exact* wording in the ad ("I know what you're thinking… this is a bullshit scam") (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:16:43], [00:17:53]).
- **Forums + review sites via AI deep research** — Reddit (he weights it highest: "where people are the most brutally honest"), Facebook groups, Twitter/X, YouTube comments, Amazon + Trustpilot reviews, Quora (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:18:22], [00:20:55]; text/18_63579552_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Master_Prompt_List.txt).
- **Macro trends** — what the whole world is talking about (e.g. AI), not just the niche; sell WITH the trend (two case studies of 2–10x revenue from re-positioning onto the AI trend) (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:26:02], [00:28:12]).
- **Sophistication/awareness state** — have these buyers bought before and been burned? Skepticism level dictates how much proof/objection-handling the copy needs (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:23:30], [00:24:27]).
- **Standing habit:** keep a personal swipe file — screen-record every great ad you see (any niche), save URLs + full-page captures continuously (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:12:05]).

## The three AI research prompts (verbatim structure in text/18_63579552_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Master_Prompt_List.txt)
All three run in ChatGPT Deep Research mode or Perplexity ⚠️DATED (Nov-2025 demo; his May-2026 Tools List moves primary research/synthesis to Claude Pro, adding Perplexity Pro only for new-niche intel, Reddit/Quora VOC mining, and cited claims — text/19_63685340_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Master_Tools_List_v2_-_May_2026_Update.txt). Run all three **in parallel tabs**, ~15–30 min each (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:18:08], [00:18:59]).
1. **ICP Deep Dive** — context/role/action prompt; attach client sales page + ad/VSL transcripts; AI first reads client materials, then mines Reddit/FB groups/IG/Amazon/Trustpilot/YouTube/forums/Quora/Twitter. Output: 10 fixed sections incl. demographics, psychographics, pain points, **minimum 20 verbatim language patterns**, objections in their own words, decision triggers, and a gap analysis (what customers say vs what client addresses) (text/18; 04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:20:13]).
2. **Competitor Analysis** — attach competitor sales pages, VSL + ad Loom transcripts (prompt warns AI the recordings are back-to-back at 2x — split the ads); AI also researches competitor reviews. Output: per-competitor profiles, saturation analysis, opportunity matrix, differentiation strategies, language opportunities. He extends it: "give me a strategy to differentiate… a unique mechanism" (text/18; 04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:25:35]).
3. **Market Research** — trends, hot topics, complaints, pain-point hierarchy, urgency factors, influencer landscape, seasonality, macro trends, opportunity gaps, timing advantages (text/18; 04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:25:46]).

Persona rule for the ICP doc: collapse to ONE named person (Agora's "Tony" figurine practice) and write only to them (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:20:45]).

## What "research complete" looks like
- **Three saved, named PDFs** — `research doc 1 ICP`, `research doc 2 competitor analysis`, `research doc 3 market` — plus the client asset folder (control-ad transcript, business details, landing page PDF, VSL transcript) (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:23:42], [00:24:57]; text/17_63673992_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Control_Challenge_Checklist.txt §1.3).
- **Two standing inputs exist:** ICP doc + client offer doc (offer, who it's for, guarantee, promise, price, payment plans). The offer doc comes from the client at onboarding or is AI-generated from their sales page/program (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:18:58], [00:19:50]; 06 [00:20:00]).
- **Human QA gate:** actually read the research docs before using them — he sanity-scans for accuracy, and when output looked thin he added more context (main-offer VSL + offer doc) and re-ran the deep-research prompts (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:23:19], [00:22:43]).
- **Timebox:** asset collection ≈ 1 hr; doc generation 20–30 min; whole research phase ≈ 1 hr 20 min on the $300 control-challenge engagement (06 [00:16:09]; text/17 §Phase 2). For a proper retainer client he scales this to a "48 hour research sprint" (24_bonus-1-the-winning-statics-playbook [00:30:52]).

## How research feeds angles and copy
- Feed the three research docs + offer doc into the LLM and ask for **50 angles → human-select top 25** ("which ones grab you"); regenerate batches until quality, add your own ideas (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:34:54], [00:35:16], [00:35:41]). 25 unique ads is his Meta-Andromeda minimum launch batch ⚠️PLATFORM⚠️DATED (04 [00:29:09]).
- Control-challenge variant: Hook Generator prompt consumes winning-ad transcript + 3 research docs (+ his ad-training slides so the AI uses HIS frameworks, not its own) → 25 hooks, each tagged with *which research insight it leverages* → Hook Selector picks top 2–3 (text/18; 06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:25:51], [00:26:00]; text/17 §Phase 3).
- In the script itself, research lands at fixed slots: hook = pattern interrupt naming the prospect's current situation; problem-agitation = **exact language from comment research**; proof + objection-handling chosen from the objections research surfaced, ideally combined in one beat (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:44:53], [00:51:06], [00:51:34]).
- Positioning payoffs he expects from the competitor doc: reviews reveal competitor weaknesses (big claims, thin proof) AND client advantages they aren't marketing — both go straight into the ads (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:24:15], [00:24:35]).

## Thin-data clients
- **His client filter avoids the problem:** the $300 challenge model targets clients ALREADY running ads with a winning control; the onboarding form requires the winning-ad file, landing page, offer overview, and top-3 competitors (ideally heavy ad spenders) up front (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:09:03], [00:09:22]).
- If the *client* is thin on data, lean on competitors: their old long-running ads + their reviews + comment sections supply the VOC and proof-of-concept the client lacks (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:04:43], [00:16:43]).
- **Cold/spec/new-niche shortcut:** skip the 2-day deep dive; run a "quick brief" prompt (paste offer/landing/notes → AI returns who it's for, awareness level, pull, promise, differentiator, proof, numbers, next step, look/feel), then model proven winners from an ad-spy tool (Foreplay ⚠️DATED: image format, still live, 30+ days running, sort by longest-running) at an 80% swipe / 20% original ratio (24_bonus-1-the-winning-statics-playbook [00:19:55], [00:20:19], [00:21:00], [00:18:14]).
- Existing fresh research (e.g. a prior week-one intel report with avatar/VOC/offer/angles) substitutes — drop the docs in and skip re-research (24_bonus-1-the-winning-statics-playbook [00:18:50]).

## Stage map (runnable)
- STAGE 0 Client intake | inputs: signed client, onboarding form (name, site, winning-ad file, landing-page link, offer overview, top-3 competitors, notes) | gate: form returned with winning ad + competitors named | outputs: business_details, raw assets list | tool: onboarding form template (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:09:03]; text/17 §1.1)
- STAGE 1 Client asset collection | inputs: winning ad video, landing page, VSL, offer doc | gate: every asset transcribed/captured + clearly file-named in one folder | outputs: control_ad_script.txt, landing_page.pdf, vsl_script.txt, offer doc | tool: TurboScribe ⚠️DATED, GoFullPage, Loom@2x (06 [00:09:38], [00:11:21], [00:11:37]; text/17 §1.2–1.3)
- STAGE 2 Competitor asset collection (~60 min) | inputs: top 2–3 heavy-spending competitors | gate: per competitor — sales page PDF, VSL recording, 10 UNIQUE ads recorded in 3 recent / 3 few-months-old / 4 longest-running split | outputs: competitor asset folder | tool: Meta Ads Library ⚠️PLATFORM + Loom@2x + GoFullPage (06 [00:13:19], [00:14:12], [00:16:09]; text/17 §2.1)
- STAGE 3 Deep-research synthesis (3 parallel runs, 20–30 min) | inputs: Stage 1+2 assets, offer doc | gate: human read-through; outputs accurate + grounded (Reddit-weighted); re-run with more context if thin | outputs: ICP doc (≥20 verbatim phrases + gap analysis), Competitor Analysis doc (opportunity matrix + differentiation), Market Research doc (trends/timing) — saved as named PDFs | tool: ChatGPT Deep Research / Perplexity ⚠️DATED (Claude primary per May-2026 list), prompts in text/18 (06 [00:17:18], [00:23:19]; text/17 §2.2)
- STAGE 4 Angle/hook generation | inputs: 3 research docs + offer doc + winning-ad transcript (+ his ad-frameworks training) | gate: human selects top 25 of 50 angles (or top 2–3 of 25 hooks); every hook traceable to a research insight | outputs: angle list / hook shortlist | tool: LLM + Hook Generator & Selector prompts (04 [00:35:16]; text/18; text/17 §3)
- STAGE 5 Research→copy injection | inputs: selected hooks + research docs | gate: problem section uses exact comment/VOC language; top objections answered with proof in-line | outputs: ad scripts | tool: scripting prompt referencing ICP + offer docs (04 [00:44:53], [00:50:50])
