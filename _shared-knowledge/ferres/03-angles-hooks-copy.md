## Angle taxonomy & generation rules
1. **Never start from a blank page.** Extract angles from the client's proven winners and competitors' long-running ads, then branch off into new angles from there (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:05:04]).
2. **Majority vs minority hooks.** Old model: one majority angle, small hook variations in front. Post-Andromeda, every fringe motive ("the little things") can carry its own ad — e.g., fitness sold via marriage, confidence, longevity, not just "lose weight" (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:24:40]). Benefits-of-benefits and hidden benefits become standalone ad angles (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:26:01]). ⚠️PLATFORM (Meta Andromeda-specific reasoning).
3. **Angle mind-map.** Brainstorm every reason a person would want the outcome; each reason = its own ad angle. His metaphor: stop hitting one piano note when there are 87 others (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [01:00:01]). An angle that's too niche maxes out its TAM — rising costs on a single hyper-specific angle is the symptom (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:59:57]).
4. **Demand capture first.** Majority of angles must target in-market prospects actively seeking solutions — capture demand, don't create it (text/18_63579552_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Master_Prompt_List.txt, Core Offer Prompt 1).
5. **Common-enemy angle** is a reliable emotional detonator for problem-aware/unaware audiences (govt, Wall Street, gurus) (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:34:43]).
6. **Avatar-callout angle.** If the offer is not for the general masses, the hook must say who it's for (and who it isn't) (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:40:32]). When the wrong people flood the funnel, over-correct with hyper-explicit callout hooks to retrain the pixel, then relax once lead quality stabilises (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:43:14]).

## Volume rules (how many, and why)
- **Launch batch: 25–35 completely different ads**, all live at once — no drip-feeding 5/week; Andromeda rewards creative diversity and the batch fatigues slower (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:29:09]). ⚠️PLATFORM.
- **Format split 5×5:** 5 straight talking-head, 5 talking-head with AI-avatar hook, 5 fully-AI, 5 B-roll, 5 testimonial/UGC — 25 unique scripts, formats deliberately varied to learn what works (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:30:48]). If client data already proves a format, weight toward it (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:33:13]).
- **Ideation overproduction:** ask AI for 50 angles, human-select the top 25 (the ones that grab YOU); if quality is thin, ask for another 50 — never feel bound to the first list (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:35:21]; deck text/deck_visuals/04_63572448_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_3_-_How_to_Create_a_Winning_Ad__pp25-36.md p.26 says "5 of each type").
- **Hook funnel (control-challenge variant):** generate 10–25 hook ideas → AI scores → select top 2 using different psychological triggers → ~3 finished "banger" ads total (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:25:28], [00:43:38]; text/18 Prompts 1–2).
- **Scene funnel:** 5 distinct video concepts per winning hook → V2V-score → 1 winner → one Sora prompt (text/18 Prompts 3–5).
- **Retainer cadence: 10 new ads/month** for ongoing optimization + fatigue replacement (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [00:07:23]).
- **Testimonial rule:** keep them raw — basic captions only, no flashy edit; the unedited feel IS the trust (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:33:54]).

## Hook rules (first 3 seconds)
1. **Stakes:** average watch decision is ~1.8 seconds; if the hook fails, nothing else in the ad exists (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:28:30]). Survive past 3s and viewers tend to stick to the end (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:30:54]).
2. **Effort allocation:** spend disproportionate effort on the hook/headline — his rule-of-thumb "~80% of your effort" even though it's one sentence (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:34:14]).
3. **Form:** 1–2 sentences max, covering the first 3–8 seconds; pattern interrupt; native to the feed, not salesy (text/18, Hook Generator). Pattern-interrupt types: shocking statement, unexpected visual, provocative question, bold claim — each must open a curiosity gap the viewer needs closed (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:36:11]).
4. **Targeting function:** the hook speaks to the prospect's *current situation or recent behavior* — Meta then finds those people (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:44:03]). Build hooks from three lenses: their **problems, circumstances, outcomes** — plus insider language only the avatar would get (a sneaky avatar callout without saying "this is for X") (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:50:39]).
5. **Five hook categories** (generate 5 per category): Shock/Controversy, Curiosity Gap ("the thing nobody tells you about X"), Social Proof ("why [group] are switching"), Fear/Urgency ("stop doing X before it's too late"), Benefit-Driven with a twist (text/18, Hook Generator).
6. **Hook selection rubric** (score 1–10 each, /60): scroll-stop power, emotional-trigger strength, audience alignment, conversion potential, algorithm/engagement potential, differentiation from the control; final 2 picks must use different emotional approaches (text/18, Hook Selector).
7. **On-screen text hook:** add a curiosity-loaded text block for the first 3 seconds on every video ad, on top of captions — it's a second simultaneous hook for sound-off scrollers (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:29:43]; reaffirmed in the demo when a finished ad "missing" one gets it added — 06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [01:18:36]).
8. **AI-avatar hook stitch:** re-shoot only the first ~5 seconds with an AI spokesperson, cut to the original talking head; one student cut cost-per-booked-call 35% this way — the avatar itself (hi-vis mine worker) was a relatability/minority-hook play (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:31:04], [00:31:56]).
9. **Statics corollary:** on static ads the headline does the hook's job — burning intrigue, an open loop the brain has to close (text/24_65967688_The_Winning_Statics_Playbook_v4_by_Sean_Ferres.txt). Details in 06-statics-playbook.md.

## The Breakthrough First Line Formula (text/16) — ⚠️ this is his COLD-EMAIL opener formula, not an ad formula
Operational detail (the disarming principles transfer to ad leads, but its native context is outreach):
- **Frame:** "hey man, you've got spinach in your teeth" — a concerned subscriber flagging a problem, friendly and disarming (text/16_63743956_THE_BREAKTHROUGH_FIRST_LINE_FORMULA.txt).
- **3 principles:** (1) Skip the handshake — write like you already have history; (2) Bar talk — speak like you would IRL, not like a LinkedIn bot; (3) For your eyes only — reader must think "this is for me and only me" (text/16).
- **Kill list:** any formal, AI-summary-flavored rapport line ("I recently came across your inspiring post…") (text/16).
- **Fallback tiers:** 2–3 universal-but-personal-sounding lines for volume; stalk recent content for dream prospects; or prompt AI to scan recent events in the prospect's life, then reword to sound human (text/16).
- **Mandatory transition sentence** after the first line explaining WHY you're reaching out, flowing as if triggered by something in your day — without it the first line reads insincere (text/16).
- **Budget rule:** ≤ a couple of minutes per first line; volume still wins (min 30 outreaches/day) (text/16).

## Copy structure & writing rules (video ad scripts)
1. **Skeleton = the 7 elements** (hook → problem agitation → solution tease → proof stack → objection handling → urgency/scarcity → CTA; anatomy in 01-foundations). Scripts must MIX element order — some lead with proof, others urgency or agitation; entertainment first, ad second (text/18, Master Viral Ad Script Prompt).
2. **Combine, don't sequence.** In a 30–60s ad the elements overlap: handle an objection WITH proof (time objection → testimonial from a busy mom-of-three); the hook itself may be the problem or the proof. "It's not a sales page… you've got to earn every second" (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:51:33]).
3. **Exact customer language everywhere.** Agitate pain with the verbatim phrases mined from comments/reviews; if comments say "scam," open with "you probably think this is a bullshit scam — meet John…" (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:17:53], [00:45:02]).
4. **Tease, never reveal:** share what it does/the transformation, not how it works — the click buys the reveal (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:45:03]).
5. **Proof hierarchy:** demonstration beats everything (bulletproof-glass CEO, Hormozi's book launch); if selling AI ads, the ad itself must BE an AI ad (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:46:14], [00:47:41]).
6. **Objections: max ~3 per ad**, woven in, prioritized from research (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:51:22]).
7. **Urgency must be real** — restructure the offer so a genuine deadline/limit exists; fabricated scarcity nukes trust and conversions (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:48:13]).
8. **CTA:** tell them exactly what to do next AND what happens after (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:49:52]).
9. **Length/format:** 30–60s scripts; 6–10 scenes of 3–8s (AI-video generation ceiling); one spoken line ≈ 4–5s; vertical 9:16; visual description per scene; on-screen text suggestion for first 3s; native platform slang (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:52:41], [01:01:58]; text/18). ⚠️DATED (8s/scene is a 2025-era Veo/Sora limit).
10. **Primary text & headline fields:** when testing a new video creative against a control, leave the existing primary text/headlines untouched — isolate the creative variable; video viewers barely read primary text anyway (statics are the opposite — text is everything there) (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [01:34:27]). ⚠️PLATFORM.
11. **Compliance constraints on copy** ⚠️PLATFORM: no direct claims with timeframes, no before/afters, avoid $-signs and trigger words; imply instead of state — let the background/visual carry the claim, Tai Lopez-style (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:56:38], [00:58:07]).

- **Winning Hook Generator / Selector** (control-challenge): 25 hooks in 5 categories, each annotated with why-it-beats-control + which research insight; selector scores /60 and returns top 2 (text/18).
- **Scene Concept Generator / Selector:** 5 standalone concepts per hook (avatar persona + environment + action + hook delivery + psychological engine; no reuse across concepts; ≥2 avatars must be occupation/lifestyle matches); V2V matrix (scroll-stop velocity, emotional impact, narrative clarity, shareability, generation fidelity, conversion potential) picks 1 (text/18).
- **Sora Scene Builder:** distill winning concept into a 50–100-word, 4-part prompt (prose scene / cinematography / actions / dialogue = the hook line) (text/18). ⚠️DATED (Sora-2-specific).
- **Master Viral Ad Script Prompt:** paste chosen angles + all docs; 5 scripts per run; "the hook determines if your ad gets 1,000 views or 1,000,000"; 7 elements, 6–10 scenes, compliance, on-screen text (text/18; walked through at 04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:50:00]).
- **Ad Scene Generator** (full-length ads): per-scene JSON output (scene_analysis, sora_prompt, duration, visual_style, transition_notes) with mood-by-scene-type guidelines (problem = darker tones … CTA = direct visuals); no text overlays (added in CapCut) (text/18).
- **Prompting hacks:** (a) Legendary-copywriter personas — "act as Gary Halbert" etc.: Halbert = story/curiosity long-form; Carlton = short punchy aggressive deadline copy; Schwartz = sophisticated/high-ticket mechanism markets; Hopkins = proof/stats for skeptics & B2B; Kennedy = contrarian "everyone else is wrong"; Ogilvy = premium/luxury credibility (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:36:54], [00:38:00]). (b) Hyperbole maximalism — demand "an ad so scroll-stopping it's physically uncomfortable to scroll past," not "a good ad" (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:50:17]). (c) Request scenes in JSON for higher-fidelity video generation (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [01:09:29]). (d) Upload the course slides with the prompt so the AI uses HIS framework, not its own (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [00:26:02]).

## Iteration rules
1. **Splice winners from data:** read per-ad retention vs CTR (Motion tool visualizes Ads Manager data ⚠️DATED) — great-hook/weak-body + weak-hook/great-body → recombine into one ad, then spin AI variants (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:06:04]).
2. **Comment mining loop:** objections in ad comments = free market research → write new ad versions answering them in the commenters' exact words (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:17:14]).
3. **Organic-to-paid:** rank client/competitor reels by views (SortFeed ⚠️DATED), take the top 10 niche-relevant, steal the hook OR format OR topic — never the whole video — and ask AI what the winners have in common, then reuse that common factor in ads (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:15:06], [00:16:04]).
4. **Hook-swapping has a budget ceiling:** different hooks on the same body is fine at test budgets, but at scale Andromeda's similarity score flags near-duplicates — scaling requires genuinely different angles, not 1% remixes (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [01:05:27]). ⚠️PLATFORM.
5. **One variable per test:** new hook vs control = same primary text, same body, one ad per ad set, clear naming ("original control" vs "challenge hook 1") (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [01:34:05]).
6. **Diagnose by funnel position:** if lead quality is wrong, the hook is what's filtering who enters — fix front-end messaging before anything else (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [00:33:55]).
7. **Refresh cadence:** creative fatigue (frequency too high, Meta deprioritises) is why the 25-ad launch + 10 new ads/month exists (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:30:13]; 05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [00:07:23]).
