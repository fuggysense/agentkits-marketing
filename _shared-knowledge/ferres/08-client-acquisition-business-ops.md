## 1. The Money Model (3-tier ascension ladder)
- Tier 1 attraction: **$300 "Beat Your Control" Challenge** (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:01:01]). Tier 2 core: **$3,000 "Andromeda Antidote"** — 25 unique creatives (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:01:28]). Tier 3 continuity: **$1k/mo "Scale Winners" retainer**, added only after delivering the core offer (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:02:07]).
- Start at $300 / $3k / $1k and raise prices as proof stacks (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:06:47]). Expected conversion: ~30% of challenge clients upgrade to the $3k core within 30 days (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:05:48]); ~1 in 3 core buyers ascend to the $1k/mo retainer (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:06:09]).
- Target: businesses already spending tens of thousands/mo on ads, so $300–$3k reads as trivial spend (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:08:16]).
- Positioning vs competition: guaranteed improvement + 7-day delivery + accessible pricing beats agencies (slow, $3–10k retainers, no guarantees) and freelancers (no systems, unreliable) (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:09:04]).
- Pricing rule of thumb at scale: ~$100 minimum per ad creative; 50 ads = $5k, 100 ads = $10k, finesse with bulk discounts (22_roast-my-ads-call-for-daniel-throssell-promo-buy [1:09:09]).

## 2. The $300 Challenge — exact offer mechanics (shapes what the ad machine must deliver)
- **Deal:** client sends their best-performing ad; you create **3 AI video hook variants** and run a 7-day head-to-head in THEIR ad account. Guarantee: "If I don't beat your best ad in 7 days, you don't pay" — full refund of the $300 (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:03:06]). Offer doc sweetener: refund PLUS a $100 steak-dinner gift card; $500 "usual" price anchored down to $300 in exchange for a testimonial (text/12_63656998_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Offer_Doc_DEMO.txt).
- **Test design (contract-fixed):** one new campaign, 4 identical ad sets — control ad + your 3 hooks — at $25/day each ($100/day total) for 7 consecutive days; client pays ad spend; winner = lowest **cost per result** for that funnel, judged solely from the client's ad-account dashboard; you win if ANY of your 3 hooks beats the control; refund within 24h if not (text/15_63720298_Beat_Your_Best_Ad_Performance_Challenge_Agreement.txt). Total client exposure ≈ $700 ad spend + refundable $300 (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:03:28]).
- **Ownership terms:** client keeps all 3 ads regardless of outcome; you keep portfolio rights + case-study rights if you win; both parties frozen from touching budget/targeting/creative mid-test (text/15_63720298_Beat_Your_Best_Ad_Performance_Challenge_Agreement.txt).
- **Hook composition rule:** of the 3 hooks, ONE is the original hook verbatim re-performed by an AI avatar, TWO are new variations; 48-hour turnaround from payment is a core selling feature (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:40:02]).
- **Delivery sequence:** Day 1 collect $300 + control ad → feed their ad script, their other Ad Library ads, and 2–3 competitors' ads into AI; build ICP/competitor/market research docs (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:39:14]). Day 11: results review call (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:42:57]). Lose → refund (or offer to keep testing free until a hook wins) — follow through on the guarantee exactly as promised (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:43:27]). Total labour ≈ 3 hrs/client; full phase-by-phase execution checklist incl. onboarding form, transcription, deep-research prompts, Sora 2 generation, CapCut stitch, client ad-setup guide: (text/17_63673992_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Control_Challenge_Checklist.txt) ⚠️PLATFORM (Sora 2 / CapCut / turboscribe specifics).
- **Friction-killer upgrade:** don't even ask for their winning ad — find it yourself in Ad Library (sort by impressions, screen-record, crop to 9:16) so the pitch becomes "all you need to do is say yes" (text/05_63574078_BODY.txt) ⚠️PLATFORM.
- **$3k core deliverable:** 25 creatives in a 5×5 grid — 5 talking head, 5 talking head w/ AI hooks, 5 full-AI, 5 B-roll, 5 testimonial/UGC — with optional 30-day "lower your ad costs 20% or money back" guarantee (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:04:40]; guarantee [0:05:32]). Demand justification: Meta's Andromeda algo update means brands need 25–50 unique creatives per campaign (23_limited-time-case-study-group [0:00:40]) ⚠️DATED (Andromeda/Q4-2026 framing).

## 3. Outreach model
- **Rule of 30:** 30 qualified prospects/day × 30 days = 900 contacts; ~3 hrs/day total (pull+qualify 60 min, DM 60–90 min, update sheet 10 min, process replies 30–60 min) (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:20:07]; [0:32:20]).
- **Lead source = Meta Ad Library** (8M active advertisers, pre-qualified because already spending, free, updated daily) over social hunting or stale lead databases (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:12:02]). Filter to Tier-1 countries (US/CA/AU/NZ/UK) or your local market; search direct-response CTAs ("Shop now", "Book a call"), lead-gen terms ("webinar", "free training"), ecom terms, or niche keywords (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:13:56]) ⚠️PLATFORM.
- **Alt source — "Reverse Ad Hack":** prime your own feed to look in-market for your target niche so their ads come to you; harvest with an ad-finder Chrome extension (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:15:26]) ⚠️PLATFORM.
- **5-star qualification:** green flags = 5+ ads running, direct-response objectives, active organic presence, ≥4.3-star reviews w/ recent proof, fast landing page with message-match + firing pixel; red flags = 1–2 ads, brand-awareness only, hypey/policy-risk claims, <4.0 rating, broken funnel. Disqualify ruthlessly (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:17:50]; [0:19:47]; checklist detail text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt).
- **Channel:** IG + FB DMs to the OWNER's personal profile, same person on both platforms (multi-channel lifts response 40–60%); max ~15 msgs/hour to avoid bans; always personalise the opener; rotate your own script variants (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:20:45]; text/11_63730620_AI_Ads_Lab_Outreach_Scripts_Follow_Ups.txt) ⚠️PLATFORM.
- **DM sequence:** (1) personalised text opener referencing their specific ad hook — ACA: Acknowledge, Compliment, Ask; (2) one generic ≤20–30s video DM pitching the challenge; (3) "cool if I send the 2-pager?" CTA → offer doc (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:23:32]; [0:25:04]; video length advice text/05_63574078_BODY.txt). Drop the price + testimonial-discount framing inside the video pitch (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:26:49]).
- **Follow-up:** 5 touches over ~14 days (nudge → offer doc → proof screenshot → demo ad → breakup), front-loaded days 2/4/7; if no hard proof yet, send "process proof" (the fair-test rules). Ghosted mid-convo → "Ghostbuster" bumps: zero-lift reminder, choice close, deadline ping, meme (highest response), assume-close scheduling, easy-out (text/11_63730620_AI_Ads_Lab_Outreach_Scripts_Follow_Ups.txt).
- **Conversation engine:** after any reply use FSA — Feel (acknowledge), Show (one line of expertise), Ask (question that moves the deal) — in 2–3 short bubbles, never a block (text/11_63730620_AI_Ads_Lab_Outreach_Scripts_Follow_Ups.txt; framework taught at 10_how-to-get-big-money-copywriting-clients-on-the- [0:14:49]).
- **KPI funnel (per 900 contacts/mo):** 12–18% engaged/request doc → 10–15% close on challenge (11–24 sales) → 20–35% ascend to $3k core → 15–25% to $1k/mo retainer (text/11_63730620_AI_Ads_Lab_Outreach_Scripts_Follow_Ups.txt). Deck variant is more conservative: ~10% reply, 5–10% close, month-1 range $4.2k–$15.5k (text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt). "Volume negates luck."
- Seasonal angle rotation: Q4 = inflation urgency; January = post-holiday fatigue recovery; rest of year = Andromeda/creative-fatigue framing (text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt) ⚠️DATED.

## 4. Objection patterns (agree first, then redirect — never argue)
Meta-rules: the guarantee is the primary weapon, use it early/often; if they balk hard at $300 they're not the client — move on; the math favours you (3 researched hooks vs 1 incumbent) (text/13_63729844_AI_Ads_Objection_Handling_Cheatsheet.txt). Key reframes (all from that doc):
- *No case studies?* → newer creator, that's WHY it's risk-reversed; trained on a proven system; zero downside.
- *Ads are fine* → "fine becomes average"; quantify: 3X→4X ROAS = 33% more profit on same spend.
- *AI slop fear* → the magic is the research, not the AI video; offer 3 hook SCRIPTS they record themselves, same guarantee.
- *Don't trust guarantees* → escalate risk reversal: escrow the $300, or pay-only-after-win in exchange for a video testimonial.
- *Has an agency* → "drop-in creative test, not a replacement; we hand them the winner; want me to loop them in?"
- *Account access* → deliver upload-ready files + setup steps; access optional.
- *$100/day too low to read* → spend more or test a lower-cost-per-result funnel (lead magnet, webinar); *too high* → disqualify politely, revisit when they've scaled.
- Sales-call posture (skim of 3h49m sales training, survey only): doctor-vs-patient — diagnose before prescribing (10_how-to-close-3k-mo-copywriting-clients-in-one-ca [1:01:53]); offer exactly two price options, no more, no less, so the choice is A-or-B not yes-or-no (10_how-to-close-3k-mo-copywriting-clients-in-one-ca [0:29:40]); with only a cheap-vs-expensive pair ~70% pick the cheaper one, so engineer a decoy ("popcorn pricing") — bump the lower tier up close to the higher tier so the expensive option reads as the better deal and ~70% flip to it (10_how-to-close-3k-mo-copywriting-clients-in-one-ca [0:31:03]; [0:31:37]); expect ~1/3 close rate on booked calls (10_how-to-get-big-money-copywriting-clients-on-the- [0:04:59]).

## 5. Contract, CRM, ascension trigger, hiring
- **Contract:** one-page challenge agreement (deal, deliverable, deposit, test setup, win condition, ownership, no-mid-test-changes) signed BEFORE work; send via PandaDoc (free) or HelloBonsai (text/15_63720298_Beat_Your_Best_Ad_Performance_Challenge_Agreement.txt; text/15_63720298_BODY.txt).
- **CRM:** Google Sheet, one row per prospect: platform, DM-1 date, next follow-up, F2–F5 dates, status pipeline `No Reply → Engaged → Challenge → Core → Subscription → Dead`, next action, notes. Untracked = unfixable (text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt; template text/14_63737680_BODY.txt).
- **Ascension trigger = BAMFAM:** mention the "results review call" at close so the upsell meeting is pre-booked; on it, show win data, explain Andromeda's 25–35-creative requirement, pitch the $3k package, offer the research docs as a sign-up bonus (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [0:33:11]; [0:44:16]; text/17_63673992_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Control_Challenge_Checklist.txt). Speed rules: respond to replies in minutes, close within 72h of doc send (text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt).
- **Hiring (thin in corpus):** scale path = months 3–5 hire VAs to DM 100+ prospects/day; months 6–9 delegate delivery via SOPs/templates and raise prices; months 10–12 run your own ads, team delivers (text/05_63574078_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_4_-_How_to_Get_Paid.txt). The "No-Brainer Hiring Kit" is a separate paid course — contents not in this corpus (text/21_65623447_BODY.txt).

## What this means for OUR ad machine
The challenge offer dictates pipeline outputs: per client it must produce (a) a buyer-research pack from the client's + competitors' live ads, (b) exactly 3 hook-swap variants of a proven control (1 faithful re-skin + 2 new angles), inside 48h, and (c) a clean 4-ad-set test spec the client can deploy without granting access. The $3k tier dictates batch capacity: 25 creatives across 5 fixed formats. Win metric is always single-funnel cost-per-result — the machine's eval gate should mirror that.
