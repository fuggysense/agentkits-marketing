## Cross-cutting rules (apply to every pattern)

1. **Hook = pattern interrupt in the first 3 seconds** — shocking statement, unexpected visual, provocative question, or bold claim; it must open a curiosity gap the viewer needs closed (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:36:11]–[00:36:23]).
2. **Master script skeleton** Sean prompts AI with: hook (pattern interrupt speaking to the in-market demographic's current situation/recent behavior) → problem agitation **in the audience's exact comment-research language** → solution tease (introduce product without revealing everything) → proof stack (≤3 social-proof items) → CTA. Beats should blend, not sit in separate boxes — handle an objection and show proof in the same line (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:50:57]–[00:51:44]). Feed it ICP + offer docs and ask for a 30–60s script (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:50:50]).
3. **Always add an on-screen text hook**: sound-off viewers give an ad ~1.8 seconds with zero context (06_part-5-full-end-to-end-ai-ad-creation-demo [01:18:36]–[01:18:42]).
4. **Raw beats polished**: unedited selfie/testimonial footage with imperfect lighting often outperforms produced ads (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:33:35]); same raw-on-purpose rule in the statics module (24_bonus-1-the-winning-statics-playbook [00:04:17]).
5. **B2B vs B2C split**: business owners → direct, credible, simple talking head, ROI-focused; general public → fast "TikTok-brain" edits, flashy visuals, entertainment value (text/03_63572440_The_AI_Ads_Lab_Pt_2_-_What_Makes_a_Winning_Ad.txt).
6. **Format pendulum**: formats fatigue as markets sophisticate, then swing back — VSLs and webinars died and now "work again." Pick patterns the niche hasn't been numbed to (03_part-2-what-makes-a-winning-ad [00:35:18]–[00:36:02]). ⚠️DATED — "VSLs work again" is a 2026 read; re-check fatigue per niche.

## The patterns

### P1 — Authority to Camera (founder / expert / celebrity / podcast clip)
- **Skeleton:** name + credential up front → teach insider specifics or speak warmly to the buyer → soft community/CTA close. Sales Recruiting Univ. proves credibility with funnel math (100 applications → screen 95 → 5 interviews → 1 hire) instead of claims (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_02.md). Dr. Squatch's is a *post-purchase welcome* ad, not a pitch — and it ran 861 days (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_01.md). Nurse Coaches is a lav-mic seated story clip, 1078 days (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_07.md).
- **Hook formula:** "Hi, I'm [name], founder of X" or an insider-numbers claim. Proof mechanism: credential + specificity. CTA: low-pressure (follow us / learn more).
- **When:** B2B/high-ticket and trust-led D2C; the longest-running ads in the vault are all this trust register. Members: VIDEO_01 Dr. Squatch (grooming), VIDEO_02 Sales Recruiting University (coaching), VIDEO_07 The Nurse Coaches (coaching), VIDEO_30 Ultimate Pet Nutrition (pets — Rob Lowe; no usable VO, celebrity variant per sheet note).

### P2 — UGC Confessional VSL (selfie problem story)
- **Skeleton (from RYZE, 168s):** rapid symptom checklist hook ("…red flag" repeated 5×, viewer self-diagnoses) → "prisoner in my own body" first-person story → failed-alternatives list (PT, massage, acupuncture) → root-cause reveal (inflammation, "everything I tried only masked symptoms") → ingredient-by-ingredient mechanism → results timeline → big social-proof number (150,000 customers) → risk-free CTA (30 days) (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_03.md). This is the master skeleton of rule 2 worn as UGC.
- **Hook formula:** second-person symptom call-outs. Proof: named mechanism + customer count. CTA: risk reversal.
- **When:** health/symptom niches at problem-aware level; taboo problems suit the confessional register (Hims, per sheet note). Members: VIDEO_03 RYZE (food & bev/supplement), VIDEO_04 Hims (telehealth — VO transcription failed; sheet rationale only).

### P3 — Full In-Feed VSL (long-form sales argument)
- **Two sub-skeletons:** (a) *Contrarian educator* (Gundry, 4.4 min): villainize familiar foods → "they're lying to you" myth-flip → gut mechanism → borrowed-story proof (stool-transplant weight gain) → segmentation quiz CTA with friction removers ("free, doesn't ask for your email") (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_12.md). (b) *Review-stack VSL* (4Patriots, 619 days): host reads named five-star reviews aloud, each one quietly killing a different objection (taste, panic-buying, "like a fire extinguisher" analogy) → urgency CTA "before this video ends" (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_13.md).
- **Hook formula:** watch-this demonstration or enemy/myth statement. Proof: story or stacked verbatim reviews.
- **When:** considered purchases, fear/health niches, colder traffic that still rewards a full argument (see pendulum rule 6). Members: VIDEO_12 Gundry MD (supplements), VIDEO_13 4Patriots (survival & prep).

### P4 — Receipts Reaction (read the customers' words)
- **Skeleton (MorningWould):** voice the skeptic's question ("does it actually live up to the hype?") → refuse to self-praise ("instead of our obviously biased answer… let's see what people are saying") → react to 3 colorful verbatim comments on screen → "these comments speak for themselves" CTA (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_15.md). Sheet note: creator reacting to a *planted comment screenshot* (text/25_65967745_Winning_Ads_Swipe_File_VIDEO.csv).
- **Hook formula:** hype-question + promise of unfiltered evidence. Proof: customer language as entertainment — funnier and more credible than brand copy.
- **When:** products with personality and quotable fans; energy/lifestyle. Member: VIDEO_15 MorningWould (supplements). Cousin of P3b.

### P5 — Show-Don't-Tell Demo & Live Reactions (incl. unboxing)
- **Skeletons:** (a) *Stranger-reaction montage* (Goat Co — Sean's "elite demo" pick): strangers try the product on camera, raw unscripted exclamations ("I can breathe!", "send me 10"), zero narration script (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_26.md). (b) *Routine-integration demo* (Therabody): occasion hook (Mother's Day gift) → consistency-is-the-real-mechanism argument → "12 minutes, hands-free" ease → limited-time sale CTA (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_25.md). (c) *Sensory unboxing* (Shaygiavanni, 1132 days): open package live, narrate delight at every detail incl. handwritten thank-you card (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_21.md). HexClad's searing-bacon demo carries no VO — visual-only, unverified here (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_24.md).
- **Hook formula:** instant visceral reaction or visual payoff in second one. Proof: product literally working on camera.
- **When:** physical products with a felt/visible effect. Members: VIDEO_24 HexClad (kitchen), VIDEO_25 Therabody (wearables), VIDEO_26 Goat Co (wellness), VIDEO_21 Shaygiavanni (skincare).

### P6 — How-It's-Made Transparency
- **Skeleton (NX1):** numeric contrast hook ("your moisturizer probably has 23 ingredients… mine has four") → walk each ingredient at the production bench → name what's NOT inside (no fillers/parabens/water) → reason-why ("water is why they need preservatives") → identity CTA ("if that's the kind of skincare you want, link below") (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_23.md). ICEGIANT = jeweler hand-crafting grillz, no VO (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_22.md).
- **Hook formula:** us-vs-industry count/contrast. Proof: watching production with your own eyes. **When:** craft/clean-label positioning vs commodity category. Members: VIDEO_22 ICEGIANT (jewelry), VIDEO_23 NX1 Project (skincare).

### P7 — Screen-Share Receipts (Loom / dashboard case study)
- **Skeleton:** promise an easy method or result ("the easiest method… literally one sentence prompt… only minutes") → live screen walkthrough of the dashboard/ads manager → CTA (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_09.md). SaaS variant: feature walkthrough with webcam bubble, mid-demo slice (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_08.md).
- **Hook formula:** "I'm about to show you" + ease/result claim. Proof: actual pixels — the screen is the receipt. **When:** SaaS, info, agency; B2B per rule 5. Members: VIDEO_08 Tribute Mgmt Software (SaaS), VIDEO_09 Mr. Paid Social (coaching).

### P8 — Street Interview / Public Stunt
- **Skeletons:** (a) *Format-as-pitch* (StreetTalk): confrontational qualifier hook ("if you're a CPG brand doing over $1M/month, Andromeda is f***ing your ad account") → "consumers don't trust influencers or scripted UGC" enemy → name-drop brands scaling with the format → mechanism (creative diversity → higher spend, lower CPAs) → book-a-call (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_10.md) ⚠️PLATFORM Meta/Andromeda framing. (b) *Absurd problem demo* (Sean): read terrible cold emails to strangers IRL → rule takeaway ("you wouldn't say it in real life, don't say it in an email") → easy segue to the better way (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_11.md + sheet note).
- **Hook formula:** confrontation or absurdity in a public setting. Proof: real strangers' responses. **When:** agencies/info selling to skeptical operators; demonstrating a "bad way" viscerally. Members: VIDEO_10 StreetTalk (coaching), VIDEO_11 Sean Ferres (coaching).

### P9 — Multi-Scene DR Skit ("Old Spice" style — Sean's flagship)
- Sean's own note: his **longest-running, most successful format ever (830 days)** — built modular, with scene order shuffled and different "baked in" headlines per variation (text/25_65967745_Winning_Ads_Swipe_File_VIDEO.csv).
- **Skeleton:** audience callout hook ("Freelance Copywriters! What do I have to do to get your attention?") → absurd visual analogy of the problem (wrong side of the golf club) → free lead-magnet offer with concrete outcomes → reason-why-it's-free ("small chance you'll conclude I'm a low-key genius") → anti-pitch humility beat ("let's not jump into bed just yet") → link CTA → fourth-wall comedy button ("Cut! Did we really have to fly to New Zealand?") (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_31.md).
- **Hook formula:** direct niche callout + spectacle. Proof: usage proof ("used by dozens to land their first clients"). **When:** lead-magnet offers to a nameable audience; personality-led brands. Member: VIDEO_31 Sean Ferres (coaching). Operationally: write it as interchangeable scene blocks so one shoot yields many ads.

### P10 — Comparison Family (before/after · us-vs-them · severity ladder)
- **Skeletons:** (a) *Expert severity ladder* (Vegamour): derm credential hook → calibrate "this much hair loss is normal / moderate / severe" with the product slotted at every tier, so every viewer qualifies for a CTA (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_32.md). (b) *Old-routine vs new-routine* (Mando): "I used to apply 3× a day… now once and I'm good till my head hits the pillow" → branded tagline CTA ("Don't mask it, Mando it") + offer stack (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_33.md). (c) *Product-vs-category split* (Magic Spoon, "Protein Bar vs Magic Spoon" — text-led, no VO; sheet-note confidence only) (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_14.md).
- **Hook formula:** "is this normal?" calibration question or relatable old-way pain. Proof: side-by-side contrast. **When:** switching offers in commodity categories; symptom-severity products. Members: VIDEO_32 Vegamour (haircare), VIDEO_33 Mando (grooming), VIDEO_14 Magic Spoon (food & bev).

### P11 — AI-Native Spectacle & Mini-Movie
- **Skeletons:** (a) *Self-demonstrating AI ad* (UgenticAI): results hook ("5 million views a month… it's not me, it's my clone — even this ad is my clone") → cost-of-old-way stack ($150k studio, 500 hours) → opt-in CTA — the creative IS the proof (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_16.md). (b) *Possibility montage* (AI Video Bootcamp, "NOT A REAL PERSON" avatar): "anything is now possible… be anyone" rapid montage → FOMO deadline ("don't start 2026 behind everyone else") → $7 low-ticket CTA (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_17.md) ⚠️DATED 2026 framing. (c) *Brand mini-movie* (Dr. Squatch × WWE): collab spectacle, scent-led wordplay, limited-drop scarcity ("before they're gone for good") (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_27.md); Liquid Death's cereal-box eruption is visual-only, no VO (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_28.md).
- **When:** B2C mass-market "TikTok brain" (rule 5); novelty buys the stop, offer does the close. Members: VIDEO_16 UgenticAI (SaaS), VIDEO_17 AI Video Bootcamp (coaching), VIDEO_27 Dr. Squatch (grooming), VIDEO_28 Liquid Death (food & bev).

### P12 — Quiz-Funnel UGC Mashup
- **Skeleton (IL MAKIAGE):** splice multiple creators' clips → benefit demo lines ("immediately blurred my pores") → quiz as the hero ("I took their 90-second PowerMatch quiz… matched me to my perfect primer") → CTA repeated ≥3× ("take the quiz right now"; "this quiz will change your life" said twice) (text/swipe_vault/VIDEO_29.md).
- **Hook formula:** superlative product claim from a real user. Proof: multiple independent voices + on-camera result. **When:** quiz/segmentation funnels — the ad sells the quiz, not the product. Member: VIDEO_29 IL MAKIAGE (beauty).

## How the vault plugs into Ferres' batch pipeline

- **Model, don't invent:** the vault exists so you pick a proven format per niche *before* scripting; each row carries format, niche, days-active (longevity ≈ profitability proxy) and ad-library link to verify it's still live (text/25_65967745_BODY.txt, text/25_65967745_Winning_Ads_Swipe_File_VIDEO.csv). ⚠️DATED days-active values are a 2026-06 snapshot.
- **Standard test batch = 25 unique scripts across 5 formats:** 5 talking-head, 5 talking-head + AI hook, 5 fully-AI, 5 b-roll, 5 testimonial/UGC — unless the client already knows a proven winner, then double down on it (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:32:55]–[00:33:21]).
- **Why diversity:** Meta Andromeda rewards creative diversity — and it's a client-pitch lever ("you need volume + variety") (05_part-4-how-to-get-paid [00:43:51]) ⚠️PLATFORM Meta-specific.
- **Cheap variation lever:** keep the proven body, swap only the hook (e.g., an AI-generated hook with a different avatar made one student's ad resonate with a new segment and run "50% more profitable") (04_part-3-how-to-create-winning-ads [00:32:23]–[00:32:50]).
- **Research outranks format:** no format choice rescues skipped audience research (24_bonus-1-the-winning-statics-playbook [00:19:01]).
