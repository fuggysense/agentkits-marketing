# Competitor Notes — SG Property Advisory / Buyer Representation

> **FICTIONAL SMOKE-TEST DATA - not a real client.** All three competitors, their ads, and the
> review excerpts below are INVENTED for the regression baseline. None are real companies.
> Captured 2026-06-11.

The category Meridian competes in isn't really "other advisors." It's the default: the free,
commissioned estate agent. The three named players below are the closest direct-model rivals.

---

## 1. SkylineEdge Property Partners (fictional)

**Model:** Commissioned agency that rebranded its buy-side service as "buyer advisory" but still
takes a 1% buyer-side commission on the purchase price.

**Ad style:** Heavy aspiration. Drone shots of condo skylines, the agent in a blazer pointing at a
balcony view. Captions like "Your dream home is closer than you think." Founder-led talking-head
reels on Instagram, lots of "DM me 'READY' to start your journey." Urgency cues everywhere — "3
units left," "launching this weekend."

**Weaknesses:**
- The "advisory" framing collapses the moment a buyer asks how they're paid. Commission = the exact
  conflict buyers in the VoC dump are scared of.
- More expensive on a S$1.2m purchase (1% = S$12,000) than Meridian's flat S$4,500, but they hide it
  as "free" because the seller's side covers it.
- All vibe, no visible analysis. They never show the spreadsheet.

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Lovely people, very polished, but I left every meeting feeling sold to, not advised. When I asked
> point blank if they earn more when I spend more, the answer got vague."
> — Google review, 3 stars, "Mei L.", 2026-03-09

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Pushed a launch unit hard. Found out later the developer was paying a bonus that quarter. Make of
> that what you will."
> — Google review, 2 stars, "Anonymous", 2026-04-01

## 2. PropData Pro (fictional)

**Model:** A subscription data tool (S$39/month) that gives buyers raw transaction data, lease-decay
calculators, and "fair value" estimates. Self-serve, no human.

**Ad style:** Fintech-flavoured. Clean dashboards, charts, "Make data-driven property decisions."
Targets the exact numbers-comfortable buyer Meridian wants. Google search ads on "URA transaction
data" and "is this condo overpriced." Tone is cool, analytical, slightly intimidating.

**Weaknesses:**
- Gives buyers MORE data, which deepens the paralysis the VoC dump screams about ("I have the data and
  I STILL don't trust my answer"). Data without a verdict doesn't remove the doubt.
- No accountability. It's a tool, not a person on your side. When you're staring at the OTP at 11pm,
  a dashboard doesn't tell you "yes, sign this one."
- Churns hard — buyers cancel after one purchase. No reason to stay.

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Great data, genuinely. But I'm not an analyst and I still couldn't tell what it MEANT for my actual
> shortlist. I wanted a conclusion, not more charts."
> — Trustpilot, 3 stars, "Wei Jie", 2026-02-22

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Cancelled after I bought. Useful for a month, no reason to keep paying."
> — Trustpilot, 4 stars, "K. Rahman", 2026-03-17

## 3. The Honest Agent SG (fictional)

**Model:** Solo "buyer's agent" who markets on a trust/anti-establishment angle but still takes a
referral split from co-broke deals — so the "no conflict" claim is half-true.

**Ad style:** Founder-as-personality. Long captions, contrarian hooks ("Why your agent is lying to
you"), TikToks ranting about industry practices. Builds trust by attacking the category, then
positions himself as the exception. High engagement, polarising.

**Weaknesses:**
- The "no conflict" promise doesn't survive scrutiny — the referral split is still a hidden incentive.
  A sharp buyer who reads the fine print feels betrayed, which is worse than never claiming it.
- One-man band. Capacity-capped, slow replies, no system. Reviews mention being ghosted mid-search.
- Trades on outrage, which attracts the angriest buyers but burns goodwill fast.

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Loved his content, that's why I hired him. Then realised he still takes a cut on the co-broke. Felt
> a bit misled given the whole 'no conflict' thing is his brand."
> — Google review, 2 stars, "Daryl", 2026-04-06

**Review excerpt (fictional):**
> "Responsive at first, then disappeared for two weeks during the most stressful part. Solo guy, just
> too stretched."
> — Google review, 3 stars, "Serene T.", 2026-03-28

## Positioning gap Meridian can own

All three leave the same opening:
- SkylineEdge has the conflict.
- PropData Pro has the data but no verdict and no person on your side.
- The Honest Agent claims clean incentives but doesn't actually have them.

**Meridian is the only one that pairs a genuinely clean incentive (true flat fee, no commission, no
split) WITH a human verdict AND visible analysis.** The line that writes itself: "The numbers, plus a
person whose paycheck doesn't move when yours does." Hold the flat-fee claim as literal and provable —
it's the one thing the competitors can't copy without rebuilding their whole business.
