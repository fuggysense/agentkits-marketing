1. **68% of Singapore residential buyers say they "weren't sure" they paid a fair price within the
   first year of purchase.**
   — Source (fictional): *Meridian Buyer Confidence Index 2026*, n=1,204 recent buyers, p.4.

2. **The median gap between a buyer's eventual purchase price and the model-estimated fair value was
   S$38,000 on resale transactions.**
   — Source (fictional): *Lim & Tan Housing Analytics, "Fair Value Drift Report" (Mar 2026)*, Table 2.

3. **74% of first-time buyers used a property portal for 3+ months before making any offer, and 41%
   described themselves as "paralysed by options."**
   — Source (fictional): *SG Homeseeker Survey 2026, Tan Hui Min et al.*, slide 11.

4. **Buyers who relied solely on a single commissioned agent were 2.3x more likely to report
   post-purchase regret than those who used an independent second opinion.**
   — Source (fictional): *National Housing Sentiment Study 2025/26, Dr. Arjun Nair*, finding 6.

5. **The average all-in cost to reverse a residential purchase mistake within 3 years (duties, levies,
   transaction costs, holding loss) was estimated at S$94,000.**
   — Source (fictional): *Meridian Cost-of-Undo Model 2026*, methodology appendix A.

6. **Only 9% of buyers said they were shown any quantitative analysis of fair value before signing the
   Option to Purchase.**
   — Source (fictional): *Lim & Tan Housing Analytics, "Pre-OTP Transparency Audit" (Feb 2026)*, p.7.

7. **Buyers offered a flat-fee advisory option chose it over a commission-based agent 3 to 1 once the
   hidden commission cost was made visible to them.**
   — Source (fictional): *Behavioural Pricing Experiment, Choo & Rajaratnam (2026)*, Exp. 2.

8. **Word-of-mouth accounted for 87% of independent buyer-advisory enquiries — paid acquisition was
   essentially absent in the category.**
   — Source (fictional): *Meridian Channel Mix Snapshot, Q1 2026*, internal.
