## The angle in one breath

You sold your flat in three weeks and the agent was a machine. Then you started buying — and the
same person went quiet. You felt it before you could name it: on the buy side, the better the deal
they get you, the less they earn. The incentive flipped. That is the whole problem, and it is fixable.

## Barrier (named first)

The buyer cannot tell whose side the advice is on, because every "expert" earns more when the buyer
spends more. On a 7-figure, decade-long decision, there is no neutral party in the room.

## Person + awareness

Solution-aware, edging product-aware. They already suspect the incentive is the problem (VoC: "less
money for them on the buy, makes sense"). They do not yet know a true flat-fee, buyer-side advisory
exists as a category. So: lead with the problem they already feel, then introduce the flat fee as the
named fix. Do not open product-aware.

## Timing (the trigger)

Their flat has sold or is about to; the clock is running on the next purchase; the agent's attention
visibly drops right when the stakes are highest. That gap is the moment this angle lands.

## Mechanism (the unique fix — Schwartz)

A flat fee, paid the same whether they buy a S$900k flat or a S$1.6m condo, with no commission and no
referral split. It is the one structure where the advisor does not profit from them spending more.
The mechanism is the message: not "trust me", but "look at how I'm paid".

## Proof (Hopkins — specificity beats adjectives)

- The visible spreadsheet: the analysis shown before they pay (mid-funnel S$290 Shortlist Teardown).
- A named, checkable fair-value gap on a real shortlist unit — a number they can re-run on URA caveat
  data themselves.
- The fee math laid bare: S$4,500 flat vs a commission they never see itemised.
- Founder credibility stated plainly, not waved: nine years buy-side analyst. (FICTIONAL — verify
  before live use; do not imply CEA agent licensing.)

## What to avoid (kill-list + brand landmines)

No "your dream home awaits", no urgency theatre, no naming or mocking commission agents, no
"guaranteed" gains, no "secret". Contrast the fee STRUCTURE, never the people.

## Emotional arc for the copy

Recognition (you felt the flip) -> naming (here's why it happened) -> relief (there's a structure
where it can't happen) -> low-risk proof (see the analysis for S$290 before you commit to anything).
