## COPY 2 (~150 words)
**HEADLINE 2:** When the agent goes quiet

Your flat sold in three weeks. The agent was a machine.

Then you started buying, and the same person went quiet.

You felt it before you could name it. On the sell side, a higher price pays them more. On the buy side, the better the deal they get you, the less they earn.

The incentive flipped. On a seven-figure decision you keep for a decade, the advice you most need is the advice they're paid not to give.
