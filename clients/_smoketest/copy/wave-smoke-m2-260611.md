## COPY 1 (~150 words — PRIMARY)
**HEADLINE 1:** The buy-side incentive flip

Your flat sold in three weeks. The same agent went quiet the moment you started buying.

You felt the reason before you could name it. On the sell side, a higher price pays them more. On the buy side, the better the deal they get you, the less they earn.

That is not a personality change. That is the structure.

The advice you most need — push for a lower price, flag the risky stack, tell you the unit is overpriced — is the advice they are paid not to give.

One structure cannot produce that conflict: a flat fee. S$4,500, the same whether you buy at S$900k or S$1.6m. Nobody earns more when you spend more.

You overpaid once. You said never again.

See the analysis on one shortlist unit for S$290, before you commit to anything.
