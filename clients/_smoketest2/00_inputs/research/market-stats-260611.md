## Awareness and sophistication read (Schwartz framework)

**Market stage:** Solution-aware to Product-aware. Buyers in this niche know supplements exist
and have tried them. The dominant failure mode is not ignorance — it is past experience with
products that didn't deliver measurable outcomes. They are skeptical, not unaware.

**Sophistication level:** Stage 3-4. "I've tried the obvious stuff. Tell me something new or
show me why yours is different in a way I can verify."

**Copy implication:** leads that open with "have you tried X" will be ignored. Leads that
acknowledge the graveyard and explain the mechanism with specificity earn attention.
