**Persona:** Supplement-fatigued US woman, 25-45, has tried multiple products, nothing stuck.
**Who they are:** Health-aware, time-poor, has a specific problem (sleep or stress), past purchases haven't solved it, skeptical of new brands, wants simple evidence over lifestyle marketing.

## THE CORE PROBLEM
- **Emotional:** Frustration and low trust. She has tried the "obvious" things and they didn't work. She suspects she's been picking the wrong products but doesn't know how to pick better ones.
- **Situational:** A bathroom shelf of half-empty bottles. A specific unresolved problem (can't sleep, stressed, low energy) that's affecting her work and relationships. No clear path forward.

## TOP 5 MOST POWERFUL EMOTIONS
1. Frustrated — she's tried things, spent money, and nothing has stuck
2. Skeptical — past promises not delivered; new brands trigger the same caution
3. Tired — literally and figuratively; low energy is both symptom and emotional state
4. Curious — she wants to understand the mechanism, not just trust a label
5. Hopeful-reluctant — willing to try again if the evidence looks different this time

## TOP 5 BIGGEST FEARS
1. Wasting money on another thing that doesn't work
2. Not knowing if something is working or not (no feedback loop)
3. Being sold a lifestyle instead of a solution
4. Committing to a subscription she can't cancel without a fight
5. Making her health problems worse by picking the wrong thing

### Micro-Persona 1: MP-01 The Graveyard Buyer

> Supplement-fatigued buyer who has tried multiple products, spent real money, and has nothing to show for it. She wants the next thing to be different — and she needs evidence, not promises.

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Motivation | Stop guessing; find something that actually works for her specific problem |
| Pain | "I have a graveyard of bottles. Nothing has worked. I don't know if I'm picking wrong or if supplements just don't work for me." |
| Desired Outcome | One kit that solves the specific problem, with a reason she can point to |
| Lifestyle / Context | Urban professional, health-aware but time-poor, has tried AG1/melatonin/Amazon buys |
| Buying Trigger | Sees a clear explanation of why this kit is different from the things she's tried |
| Awareness Level | Solution-aware (knows supplements exist, has tried them) |
| Sophistication Level | L4 — jaded; needs mechanism + checkable proof |
| What Makes This Segment Distinct | Past failure pattern is the dominant filter; needs the skepticism acknowledged before any promise lands |
| Source Confidence | High — VOC quotes 1, 3, 8, 19, 24, 25 all point here |

#### Psychology
- **Primary emotion:** Frustration / skeptical hope
- **Primary fear:** Wasting money again; another product with no measurable outcome
- **Raw inner dialogue:** "I've tried this before. Why would this be different? But if the explanation is actually specific..." (VOC Q21, Q20)

#### Market Behavior
- **Past solutions tried:** AG1, Amazon magnesium, melatonin, ashwagandha, probably Ritual
- **Why those fell short:** No specificity, no explanation, no feedback loop
- **What breaks through:** Specific mechanism + form/dose rationale + honest trial option before subscription

| Awareness Level | Solution-aware edging product-aware |

| What Makes This Segment Distinct | The "tried addressing it as the wrong problem" insight is the unlock; not the supplement itself |
