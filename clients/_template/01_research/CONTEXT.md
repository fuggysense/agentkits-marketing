# 01_research — Research Contract

Use this stage to turn raw client, website, product, market, competitor, and existing-asset material into research that downstream concept and script work can trust. Stable or settled research gets promoted into `_swipe/research/` or distilled into `_brand/`.

## In scope

- Competitor deep-dives beyond playbook benchmarks
- New audience surveys / interviews
- Market awareness and sophistication research
- Buyer language, objections, category claims, and compliance-sensitive claims
- Existing asset audits, including videos, product shots, transcripts, and prior prompts
- Ad-hoc question-driven research that doesn't fit the swipe reservoirs
- Scrape outputs awaiting synthesis

## Out of scope

- Long-term swipe files → `_swipe/research/`
- Brand identity decisions → `_brand/`
- Concept generation → `02_script/`
- Input image generation, beat sheets, or render prompts → `03_production/`

## Output convention

Each artifact → `output/<YYMMDD>-<topic>.md`

Required when starting a new paid video campaign:
- `output/<YYMMDD>-market-awareness.md`
- `output/<YYMMDD>-buyer-language.md`
- `output/<YYMMDD>-existing-asset-audit.md`
- `output/<YYMMDD>-claims-and-compliance-notes.md`

## Hand-off rule

When research stabilizes into reusable reference, promote it to `_swipe/research/` or distill it into `_brand/`. Do not let `02_script` consume raw source dumps when a compact research summary should exist.

## Skills available for this phase

**`yt-ideator`** (global) — YouTube concept generation using the Linden Chasteen method: TAM-first thinking, niche positioning, concept scoring, and title testing. Use when the output target is YouTube long-form and you need validated ideas before scripting.

Trigger phrases: "youtube ideas", "yt ideation", "concept ideas for youtube", "what should I make next", "ideate for youtube", "video topics", "title test", "TAM for this channel"

Output feeds directly into `output/<YYMMDD>-youtube-concepts.md` for handoff to `02_script/`.
