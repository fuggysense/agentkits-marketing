## Design decisions (why the gate is built the way it is)

- **Source resolution order:** (1) explicit `claims:` ledger in the dct (authoritative,
  claim -> source path + anchor), (2) prices/offsets in `_brand/offer.md` (whitelist),
  (3) auto-trace through the client's `00_inputs/`, `_swipe/research/`, `_brand/` and
  `~/AI workflows/research-vault/`. First hit wins.
- **False-positive control (the hard part):** a bare percentage like "73%" matches almost any
  property corpus by chance. Three layers stop that:
  1. Vague quantifiers ("thousands", "most") carry no digit, so they are never extracted as
     checkable claims (this is why Eugene's A01 "thousands of homeowners" phrase is correctly
     not flagged).
  2. Years, image dimensions, and layout percentages ("top 28% of the frame") are suppressed
     at extraction.
  3. Auto-trace only treats a 4+ digit currency figure as distinctive enough to stand alone.
     Everything else (percentages, ratios, multipliers, small currency) must co-occur with
     the claim's distinctive SUBJECT words within ~200 chars of the number in the same file —
     two distinct subject words for soft stats, one for small currency. Art-direction
     vocabulary ("Straits Times business feature statistic spreadsheet") is in the stopword
     list so image-prompt styling words cannot anchor a trace against a research SOURCE index.

## Things noticed but NOT fixed (logged, out of scope per "surgical" rule)

- **Auto-trace provenance is best-effort, not authoritative.** The trace answers "does this
  number exist in the research corpus near its subject", not "this exact file is the canonical
  source." For example, Eugene's `$200,000` first-hit lands on an unrelated IG profile JSON that
  happens to contain the string, even though the real source is the positioning-call transcript
  (line 32, "a negative sum of coming close to 200k"). The verdict (sourceable) is still correct;
  the cited path is just the first match, not necessarily the best one. The `claims:` ledger is
  the authoritative path when provenance precision matters. Acceptable for a gate; flagged for
  awareness.
- **`2.5%` resolves as sourced via auto-trace.** It is a real, well-attested CPF rate and the
  match is genuine (co-occurring "accrued interest / compounded"). But a stricter policy could
  make percentages ledger-only. Left as-is because forcing a real rate to UNSOURCED would be a
  false positive that erodes operator trust in the gate. If the team prefers percentages to be
  ledger-only, that is a one-line policy change in `resolve_source`.
- The smoke `dct.json` uses a per-DCT `image_pool` shape that deviates from the canonical flat
  top-level pool (already logged in the smoke `_provenance.deviations`). The gate handles both,
  so no action needed.
