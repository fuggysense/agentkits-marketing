## Chairman's Verdict

### Where the Council Agrees

Four of five advisors (Contrarian, First Principles, Executor, Outsider) agree most "gaps" are renames of existing primitives: Enemy = Schwartz Channeling, Mechanism Education = existing Phase 3, False-Solution = Sophistication Stage 4, Bundled-Promise = Hormozi Stack, Dual Future-Pace = 6 Emotional States end-state. Four of five say skip the 10-State Arc — theory bloat with no canonical source. Three reviewers (1, 2, 3) independently flag the sample-size-of-one problem: n=1 supplement VSL is not signal for a 5-industry pipeline. Two advisors plus two reviewers converge on the meta-diagnosis: this is an audit/detection problem, not a framework-inventory problem.

### Where the Council Clashes

**Build vs. diagnose.** Executor and reviewers 4/5 say ship Monday — Files 1+2 in 2 hours, falsifiable test. First Principles, Contrarian, and reviewers 1/2/3 say diagnose first — adding 5-7 files when the underlying primitives already cover the territory creates audit fatigue and tool-selection ambiguity for future-Jerel.

**Enemy/Villain generalizability.** Expansionist argues it's the highest-leverage move precisely because B2B avoids it. Contrarian and reviewer 5 say weaponizing enemies in SG property gets you a MAS complaint, not differentiation; B2B buyers read fearmongering as manipulation. Reasonable disagreement because tonal calibration is genre-dependent.

**Vertical overlay vs. category sensitivity tier.** Expansionist wants a 100-letter teardown library across 10 verticals. Contrarian wants one field in Phase 1 ("category sensitivity tier"). Same diagnosis, opposite scale. Expansionist's version is a 6-month project; Contrarian's is a 10-minute edit.

For this question — system architecture and cross-industry generalization — **Contrarian and First Principles dominate**. Executor's veto matters most on *what ships first*, not whether to ship the full 7.

### Blind Spots the Council Caught

- **Reviewer 1: regulatory dialect.** Supplement VSL moves are FTC/FDA scar tissue, not persuasion innovations. Porting them to SaaS/property imports manipulation-coded patterns into unregulated categories. No advisor saw this.
- **Reviewer 2: corrections.md diff.** If these 7 gaps haven't been logged as corrections on prior letters, they haven't earned framework status. The audit working as designed produced output, not a pipeline gap.
- **Reviewer 4: detector failure.** The audit didn't flag these on prior letters — that's a reviewer-coverage bug, not a parts-catalog shortage. Fix the detector before expanding the catalog.
- **Reviewer 5: conversion data.** Nobody asked if Zinc7 actually converted. These 7 elements might be correlation, not causation.

### The Recommendation

**Do NOT build 5-7 new framework files.** The majority diagnosis is correct: 5 of 7 are renames, 10-State is bloat, and the audit instrument is the real bug. But don't stop at the Contrarian's single-field fix either — that ships nothing testable.

Three-step sequence:

1. **Diff before build.** Pull `corrections.md` for sales-letter-method and sales-letter-audit. Check which of the 7 gaps were ever flagged on real client work. Anything not logged = sample-size-of-one. Drop it.
2. **Fix the detector, not the catalog.** Add discrete-artifact gates inside existing phases (First Principles' prescription): Phase 2 must output "named adversary" string; Phase 3 must output "false-solution discredit block"; Phase 1 must output "category sensitivity tier" (Contrarian's field). These are reviewer gates, not new frameworks.
3. **Teardown 3-5 winners per industry before generalizing** (Reviewer 3). One Zinc7 doesn't justify pipeline changes for SG property + B2B SaaS + services + ecom.

Skip Expansionist's 100-letter library — maintenance graveyard. Skip Executor's Phase 2.5 Mechanism Education — it's already Phase 3.
