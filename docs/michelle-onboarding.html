The big idea (borrowed from people who've done it)

Two operators we've studied carefully:

Iman Gadzhi says

"Organic isn't a sales channel — it's a trust accumulation system. People buy because they feel safer, not because they understand more."

Implication for you: stop trying to convert from a single post. Each post adds a brick to a wall of trust. The wall is what closes the bootcamp.

Alex Hormozi says

"The bigger the plane, the longer the runway has to be. 78% of our top customers consume 2+ long-form pieces — about 7 hours — before buying."

Implication for you: at $1,997 for the bootcamp, short-form alone won't close it. Your workshop replay (Phase 7, when it exists) becomes the load-bearing trust-builder. Phase 1a (weeks 1-2): short-form runs soft CTAs only — "save this," "reply if this is you." Phase 1c onward (after we build the LinkedIn Trust Leak Audit PDF in week 1-2): short-form drives "DM AUDIT" → audit landing page → email gate → PDF.

What you do (your part)

SETUP · ONCE

1 · Take a 30-minute voice interview

Jerel runs you through ~15 questions about how you talk, what you stand against, what makes you cringe, what you love saying. We use this to build your voice files so the AI never sounds like a robot impersonating you.

SETUP · ONCE

2 · Walk through 6 funnel-goal questions with Jerel

Six questions about your offer, where your funnel is leaking, and what outcome you want in 90 days (Q6 — pillar comfort — is optional). Verbal in weeks 1-2 (paper-and-pen). On the dashboard once it ships (earliest week 5, conditional). This tells the system whether to make more attention-grabbing posts, more proof-driven posts, or more direct-offer posts each week.

Retakeable any time you want to recalibrate.

SETUP · ONCE (with Jerel)

2b · Build your T1 lead magnet

"LinkedIn Trust Leak Audit" — a 1-page PDF where 7 trust leaks get scored 1-10. We co-design it in week 1 so by week 2 there's a real destination for the "DM AUDIT" CTAs. Without this, BOFU posts have nowhere to point.

Email capture is mandatory — ConvertKit / Beehiiv / MailerLite landing page. Jerel sets this up; you pick the email tool.

WEEKLY · ~30 MIN

3 · Record a 5-minute voice memo

Once a week, talk to your phone for 5 minutes about whatever's top-of-mind — a client win, a story, a frustration with the industry, a hot take. Weeks 1-4 (and any time before dashboard ships): email or share the audio with Jerel. Once the dashboard ships (conditional): drop into the dashboard directly. Either way, we mine it for 3-5 fresh content ideas in your raw voice.

Iman calls this "raw content" — and his data says coaches who skip this step start sounding generic by month 3.

3× PER WEEK · ~5 MIN EACH

4 · Approve each post before it ships

For each of the 3 posts: read the hook + caption + see the visual, and approve. Or reject with a one-line reason ("hook feels too aggressive" / "I'd never say that word").

Until the dashboard ships: in a shared Google Doc. After: in the dashboard. Either way, every rejection teaches the system. Target: rejection rate trends down toward zero — actual pace depends on how cleanly your voice gets captured in the interview.

3× PER WEEK · ~10 MIN EACH

5 · Post to IG / LinkedIn yourself

When a post is approved, you get a shared Google Drive folder (until dashboard ships) or a dashboard link (after) with the caption, the asset, and the recommended posting time. You upload to IG / LinkedIn manually (so the algorithms reward native uploads).

URGENT · <5 MIN TARGET

6 · Respond fast to hand-raise signals

When someone DMs "AUDIT", comments "YES", or replies meaningfully to a story — reply within 5 minutes if possible. Hormozi's data shows this lifts conversion up to 391%.

Until dashboard ships: you watch your own DMs / comments (realistic baseline). After: dashboard surfaces them in-app (no email push). Automated keyword-reply DMs and Telegram pings come in Phase 6+.

Your total weekly time

~75 min/week at the start = 1 voice memo (5min) + 3 post approvals (15min) + 3 manual uploads (30min) + DM responses (25min).

Honest caveat: if the content works and DM volume grows, your DM time grows with it. By month 2 the realistic steady-state is closer to 90–120 min/week. The pipeline saves you the strategy + scripting + production hours (which would be 8–15/week if you did it yourself); your time is concentrated on your voice, your approvals, and your audience. We re-check this at week 3.

What we do (Jerel + the system)

Idea sourcing — 3 ways, all merging into one weekly pool

  • 🔍 Outlier scraping — top-performing posts from other coaches/consultants worldwide (weekly)
  • 🎙 Your voice memo — mined for 3-5 ideas (weekly)
  • 💡 Your offer × buyer profile — angles AI generates from your locked positioning

Weekly scheduling — picks 3 posts that hit two balances at once

  • Funnel split: 60% attention-grabbing · 30% trust-building · 10% direct-offer
  • Pillar split: 40% Educate · 30% Sell · 20% Story · 10% Inspire
  • Variety constraint: 1 hook test + 1 proof-driven + 1 pain/objection every week

If you drop a "must-do" idea, it overrides the math and slots into the right cell.

Voice-locked scripting + caption + visual brief

Hook → body → CTA — all drafted in your voice (from the 30-min interview). You can edit any hook inline before approval, or just approve as-is.

Performance loop — every Friday

You screenshot IG Insights for each post + paste numbers into a shared Google Sheet (takes ~5 min). Winners (top hook rate × hold rate) get reverse-engineered on a Friday call. Their structure feeds next week's posts. Losers get diagnosed (was it hook? topic? CTA?). When IG Insights API access lands, this becomes automatic.

Repurposing — every winning post becomes 3-5 children

When something hits, we don't move on — we mine it. One IG Reel becomes a LinkedIn post + a carousel + a tweet thread + a same-platform re-cut. When your workshop replay drops, it spawns 5-8 short-form clips for the next month.

Rejection rate trends down. Hook rate trends up. First "winner" surfaces (top hook rate × hold rate). We decompose it together — what worked, what to repeat. Week-4 success target (hypothesis): signal quality is improving, at least one content winner is identified, and DM/call movement is measurable in the analytics sheet. We are NOT promising a specific booked-call count by week 4 — without baseline conversion data from your current channels, any number would be a guess.

Weeks 5-12 — compounding

Repurposing pipeline kicks in. Each winner spawns 3-5 children. Your content compounds. Workshop spec gets built in parallel. Aim by month 3: a content engine that runs at ~75-90 min/week of your time, with measurable DM movement. Actual compounding rate depends on how cleanly your voice was captured and how fast the audit landing page converts cold readers.

Beyond month 3 — watch the failure modes

We start watching for the 3 failure modes Iman flagged: views without buyer intent (month 3), lead quality collapse (month 6), founder-dependence (month 12). The system has built-in diagnostics to flag them early — we surface them with you in the Friday review so you can decide what to change.

The things we're explicitly NOT promising

  • We're not promising views. Iman: short-form is a filter, not a closer. The right viewer is worth more than reach.
  • We're not promising overnight DMs. Hormozi: $1,997+ tickets need ~7 hours of trust accumulation. Real signal lands in weeks, not days.
  • We're not promising automation does the heart work. The voice memo, the approval taste, the 5-min DM response — those are you. Always you. The system multiplies your judgment; it doesn't replace it.
  • We're not promising the workshop ships in MVP. The workshop replay + nurture flow are Phase 7. Content runs against the T1 PDF + email gate that we build in week 1.
  • We're not promising a dashboard on any specific week. Weeks 1-4 run on shared Google Docs + Google Sheet. The dashboard arrives after we've proven the loop works for you manually AND Jerel's CLI orchestrator is stable. Earliest realistic = week 5. If the manual loop surfaces structural issues, the dashboard slips with them.
  • We're not promising IG-Insights-API analytics in MVP. You'll screenshot weekly until your IG Business / Graph API access is set up. Numbers are real either way; entry is just manual.

How your data is handled

Plain-English version. (Operator plan has the full table.)

  • Voice memos + transcripts: stored in your shared Google Drive (weeks 1-4), then in here.now (the pilot's private storage). 12-month retention. You can ask for delete any time.
  • DMs + comments from your audience: summarized only — we track themes and counts, not individual messages. Raw text never leaves the Friday review unless you say "let's save this one as case study material."
  • Your customers' info (case studies): only used in content with their written consent. No exceptions.
  • Analytics screenshots: in your shared Drive folder, 12-month retention.
  • Your data is yours. Request a full export or full delete any time — Jerel ships it within 7 days.
  • No third-party AI sees your audience's identifying info. Subagents see themes; they don't see names or handles.
  • Singapore PDPA-aware. Standard practices apply: data used only for your content production, reasonable retention, deletion on request.
