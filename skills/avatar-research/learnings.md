## Confirmed Patterns

### 16-Point Breakdown (260418 — extended from 12)

**Rule:** every avatar gets 16 dimensions, not 12. The original 12 cover Schwartz awareness/sophistication mapping (uniquely valuable, unique to this skill). The 4 added dimensions cover psychological depth: Top 5 Deep Fears, Raw Inner Dialogue, Desired Transformation, Relationship Impact (family/work/self-worth).

**Why:** the original 12 were tactical (what ads work). The 4 new dimensions are psychological (who they are inside). External research validation showed avatar files without psychological depth produced creative that read as sympathetic-but-distant — accurate but not gut-wrenching. The new dimensions force the synthesiser to ground in private-context buyer language (anonymous confessions, late-night posts, deleted comments) where the actual emotional truth lives.

**How to apply:** when filling in dimensions 13-16, source verbatim where possible. If verbatim isn't available, mark `[inferred from pattern]` and cite the 5+ public quotes the inference draws from. Never invent — that violates the data-reliability rule.

**Use rule for ad creative:**
- Top 5 Deep Fears = background tension in cold creative; direct naming reserved for warm/retargeting
- Raw Inner Dialogue = UGC scripts (creator says it on camera, NOT brand voice)
- Desired Transformation = desire-led angles + transformation VSLs
- Relationship Impact: family/work zones in cold; self-worth zone in retargeting only
