## Psychological Depth (added 260418)

> The 4 dimensions below capture what the buyer wouldn't openly say + what life looks like if the problem dissolves + how the problem ripples through family/work/self. Source: verbatim Reddit/forum quotes + private-context language patterns. If you can't ground a claim in a quote, mark `⚠️ NEEDS BUYER VALIDATION` — never invent.

### 13. Top 5 Deep Fears
> Ranked. Secret. The things they wouldn't openly admit at a dinner party. Source verbatim where possible.

1. [Fear] — Source: "[verbatim quote]" — [origin]
2. [Fear] — Source: "[verbatim quote]" — [origin]
3. [Fear] — Source: "[verbatim quote]" — [origin]
4. [Fear] — Source: "[verbatim quote]" — [origin]
5. [Fear] — Source: "[verbatim quote]" — [origin]

**Use in ads:** these fears should NEVER be named directly in cold creative (too aggressive — feels like manipulation). Use as the BACKGROUND tension that hooks reference obliquely. Direct fear-naming is reserved for warm/retargeting where trust exists.

### 14. Raw Inner Dialogue
> The voice in their head when no one is listening. NOT the curated self-image (that's #3). NOT the public persona. The actual sentences that loop in their head at 11pm or in traffic.

Examples (replace with avatar-specific):
- "Why is this so hard for me when other people seem to figure it out?"
- "I should have done this 5 years ago."
- "If I bring this up again with [partner/colleague], they're going to roll their eyes."
- "Maybe I'm just not the kind of person who [does X]."
- "Everyone else seems to know something I don't."

**Source rule:** raw inner dialogue is the HARDEST dimension to source — buyers don't post their inner monologue verbatim. Look for: deleted-Reddit-comment threads, anonymous confession formats (r/confession, anon Twitter alts), late-night DM screenshots in support groups, voice-message transcriptions in private podcasts. If you can't find verbatim, INFER ONLY from compound patterns across 5+ public quotes — and mark as `[inferred from pattern]`.

**Use in ads:** UGC scripts where the creator says these lines on camera (NOT a brand voice saying them) — peer-to-peer recognition. "I'm-you" hook entry.

### 15. Desired Transformation
> The "magic genie" outcome. What life looks like if the problem fully dissolves. Concrete, sensory, specific. NOT "I'd be happy" — "I'd close my laptop at 6pm and go to my daughter's recital without a knot in my stomach."

- **Daily life:** [what changes in the morning routine, the work day, the evening]
- **Relationships:** [what changes with partner/kids/colleagues/peers]
- **Self-image:** [what they get to stop thinking about themselves]
- **External markers:** [what others would notice — house, schedule, body language, calendar]
- **Internal markers:** [what they would notice that no one else sees]

**Use in ads:** desire-led angles + transformation-led VSLs. The MORE concrete this is, the better the ad converts. Generic transformation ("more time, more freedom") = no resonance. Specific transformation ("Sunday meal-prep no longer takes 4 hours; you get those Sundays back") = resonance.

### 16. Relationship Impact
> How the unsolved problem ripples through three explicit zones. Each zone gets a verbatim quote where possible.

- **Family:** [how it affects partner / kids / parents / extended family] — Source: "[quote]"
- **Work:** [how it affects performance / colleagues / career trajectory / income] — Source: "[quote]"
- **Self-worth:** [how it shapes their identity / confidence / self-talk] — Source: "[quote]"

**Use in ads:** the family/work zones are common in cold creative. The self-worth zone is reserved for warm/retargeting (too vulnerable for cold). Map each zone to a different angle — family-impact angle for one DCT batch, work-impact for another, self-worth for retargeting only.
