# Offer Validation (George Ten Method)

Test demand with real money BEFORE building the product. One direct ad → stripped sales page → purchase objective → read the funnel where it breaks. Profitability is NOT the goal of the test — clean data is.

## The non-negotiables (memorize)

1. **Purchase objective. Highest volume. No bids. Say no to every Meta AI suggestion.**
2. **$15/day minimum, 48 hours minimum.** Don't touch it mid-flight. Early stop allowed only if CTR All is low AND zero ATCs after 24h.
3. **Broad targeting. Tier 1 only: US, UK, CA, AU. Feeds only** (FB feed, FB profile feed, IG feed, IG profile feed). No interests, no lookalikes, no stories/reels.
4. **One ad = one image, one primary text, one headline.** No flex ads — you must know which part broke. Run 1 ad, or throw 3–5 ads into one ad set and let Meta decide (George Ten voice note, 260613) — as long as each ad keeps one variable.
5. **Ad image: no text, doesn't reveal the offer** — it exists to make them read the copy.
6. **Ad copy: direct, no clever angles — but withhold the price and the full pitch while testing.** Name what it is and open a curiosity gap so they must click Learn More to get the rest. Price + guarantee live on the sales PAGE, not the ad. Putting both on the ad lets a buyer decide from the feed: if they don't want it they scroll past and you never see why; if they do, they click without ever needing the page. Either way you lose the link-click data point. Hiding the price is what keeps that click readable (George Ten voice note, 260613).
7. **CTA button: "Learn More"** — don't change Meta defaults.
8. **Sales page:** no button in first scroll, no video, price next to the button, buy-word on the button ("Get Access — $X", never "Learn More").
9. **Tracking: two independent sources minimum** (Meta Pixel + platform analytics) + Microsoft Clarity on the page.
10. **3 sales at good conversion rate = validated.** ~33 link clicks = statistically complete test.

## Hard caveats

- This setup is for TESTING ONLY — it will not scale (warm audience exhausts in 48h). Scaling = all placements, angles, T1+ → worldwide.
- Ignore CPC and CPM entirely — they will look terrible by design.
- Offers >$100: method prefers <$97 front-end (ideally $17–27). $100 is a psychological barrier — go as cheap as possible; if you can't reach $17/$27, still come in under $100, charm-priced (97/99), the way retail stacks items just below the line (George Ten voice note, 260613). Anything at or above $100 is outside impulse-buy range on Meta. Higher tickets work but expect lower volume; consider a bait product or content-led funnel instead.
- Single-country businesses (e.g. SG, India): substitute top-tier cities of that country for T1 countries.
- No product yet? Sell as-is, then email buyers: refund or wait for delivery. Never use waitlists or fake sold-out pages — they don't produce conclusive data.
