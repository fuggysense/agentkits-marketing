# Heatmap Analytics Checklist (page diagnosis)

Source: georgeten.com/materials/checklist-heatmap-analytics.html
Use when ads look good but nothing converts. Microsoft Clarity, two questions, in order.

## Question 1 — How many mobile visitors REACHED the Add to Cart button?

If most never scroll deep enough to see the button, click rate is meaningless.
Low reach = the PAGE isn't holding them — something above the button kills the read (headline, length, image, price shown too early). Not an offer problem.

## Question 2 — Of those who reached it, how many CLICKED?

Only ask once reach is healthy. Reached-but-didn't-click = page worked, OFFER didn't (price, perceived value, button copy).

**The #1 perceived-value lever:** "this isn't applicable to my specific situation." Fix by adding, above the button, explicit proof it works for their niche/industry/case ("works for info products, devs, physical products, job seekers…"). Then the usual: bonuses, guarantee, price anchoring.

## Finding the numbers in Clarity

1. clarity.microsoft.com → your site → Heatmaps
2. Filter Device = Mobile (FB traffic is mobile; desktop muddies the read)
3. Scroll heatmap → find depth of first ATC button (often ~85%) → note % of users reaching that depth → × sessions = people who saw the button
4. Click heatmap → clicks on the ATC button
5. Clicks ÷ reachers = click-to-ATC rate
6. Cross-reference with Meta Pixel ATC events + platform checkout views

## Interpreting

| Scroll reach to button | Signal |
|---|---|
| Low | Page problem — fix above-the-button content |
| High | Read is fine — problem is offer/button |

| Click-to-ATC (mobile) | Signal |
|---|---|
| <10% | Offer or price problem |
| 10–15% | Page working |
| 15–20%+ | Incredible |
