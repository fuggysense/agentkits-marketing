## Patterns Under Test

*(Hypotheses drawn from Hormozi consultation — NOT validated yet)*

- **Cover page with Dream Translation + Calculator Close** is expected to anchor value perception before the client flips to page 2. Test: do clients quote back the dollar figure during the call? If yes → working.
- **Primary Constraint single-flag** is expected to simplify the conversation — clients focus on ONE thing instead of five. Test: do clients stay on the constraint dimension during Q&A or do they drift to other dimensions?
- **Plus/Minus Map on page 6** is expected to heighten motivation without making clients feel pressured. Test: do clients lean in or lean back during this page?
- **BAMFAM at the end of page 7** is expected to make next-meeting booking frictionless. Test: does Jerel end every onboarding call with a calendar slot committed, or do some clients leave without one?

## Open Questions

- Should the 5-dimension rubric be the same across all verticals, or do some verticals need dimension swaps? (e.g., e-com might need "Creative Iteration Velocity" instead of "Funnel Architecture")
- What's the right upper bound on Black Book perceived value? Too low and it feels weak; too high and it feels like fake math.
- How do we handle clients where the primary constraint is genuinely "budget too small"? (Awkward to tell someone "pay us and also raise your budget")
- Should the PDF include client's current ad spend as a stated input on the Cover page, or keep it in the appendix? (Hormozi is ambiguous on this)
