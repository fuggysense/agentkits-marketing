# Phase 0.5 — Claim Audit

## What this file teaches

Before any drafter writes a word, sort every claim the letter might make into three buckets. The drafters write better, faster, and cleaner copy when they know what they cannot say. Fixing overclaim in Phase 3 is expensive — half the draft has to be rewritten. Naming the limits up front means Phase 1 produces clean copy on the first pass.

## The three buckets

Produce a one-screen claim inventory the drafters must obey:

- **CAN claim** — settled science, product specs, third-party-tested data, named outcomes from real clients with documented results, and credentials or certifications the operator holds.
- **CANNOT claim** — anything that reaches past the research; brand-name competitor comparisons that break ad-platform rules; red-flag claims in health, money, or legal copy; made-up testimonials or invented proof.
- **NEEDS CAREFUL WORDING** — early-stage research dressed up as settled fact, a mechanism guess presented as proven, outcomes against the operator's own past selves, and claims that need more context than the letter has room to give.

## When this matters most

This step is critical for health, money, wellness, legal, and any field with rules about what you can say in an ad. For unregulated fields (consulting, agency, coaching, B2B software), the audit is lighter. Focus on two things: do not promise guaranteed outcomes, and do not name competitors.

## What it produces

The output is a single-screen "what you can and cannot say" document. Hand it to every Phase 1 drafter together with the component matrix. The drafters use it as a hard constraint, not a suggestion.

## Why this runs before Phase 1, not after

Drafters write differently when they know the limits. A drafter who knows "we cannot promise weight loss in 30 days" will reach for a different sentence shape. A drafter who finds out in Phase 3 has already built half the letter around the claim, and the rewrite cost is huge.

## Assertions a reviewer can score

- The CAN bucket lists at least five concrete claims with the proof source named. *(yes / no)*
- The CANNOT bucket names every competitor mentioned in the offer or buyer-profile docs and marks them off-limits for brand-name comparison. *(yes / no)*
- For health, money, or legal verticals: every CANNOT entry maps to the rule it would break (ad-platform policy, regulator, or platform-specific). *(yes / no)*
- The NEEDS CAREFUL WORDING bucket has at least one entry, or the auditor has written one sentence on why none apply. *(yes / no)*
- The document fits one screen. *(yes / no)*
