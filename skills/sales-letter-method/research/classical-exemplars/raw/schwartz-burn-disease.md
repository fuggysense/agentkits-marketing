# Eugene Schwartz — "Burn Disease Out of Your Body" (20-Year Control)

**Source:** swiped.co/file/burn-disease-ad-by-eugene-schwartz/ (image scan + Schwartz's own commentary)
**Year:** Originally written 1979, ran for 20 years as both direct mail and full-page print ad. Still mailing increased volumes 8+ years after Schwartz's death.
**Format:** Long-form advertorial (looks like an editorial article)
**Vertical:** Health / alternative medicine (Chinese self-massage book)
**Status:** Ran for two decades as the unbeatable control on a self-published book. Schwartz quoted saying: "We paid the author well over a million dollars... I never thought the thing would sell. Layout, letter, very crude, very small type. A lot of violations."

## Headlines (visible from the ad scan)
> **How modern Chinese Medicine helps Burn Disease Out of Your Body**
>
> *(Subhead in earlier mail version)* "Burn Disease Out of Your Body Laying Flat on Your Back, Using Nothing More Than the Palm of Your Hand"

## Schwartz's own commentary
> "We paid the author well over a million dollars. I wrote it as a special favor for the author because he had done a special favor for me. He gave me back the use of my right hand after I had my stroke.
>
> I paid $125 for the layout. It's as crude as can be. I never thought the thing would sell. Layout, letter, very crude, very small type. A lot of violations.
>
> I couldn't get anybody to run it, so I started my own business to run it. 'Burn Disease Out of Your Body Laying Flat on Your Back, Using Nothing More Than the Palm of Your Hand' are not my words. They're the author's words.
>
> I wrote seven paragraphs of this letter. But I had the ability to let this man speak for himself. And he still speaks to millions of Americans. We are mailing more in January than we mailed for the first eight years of the mailing. And it goes against very, very strong, strong, strong wonderful copy."

## Structural notes (from ad-image inspection)
- Headline is a *promise* but mechanism-bearing: "modern Chinese Medicine" is the named mechanism, "burn disease out of your body" is the visceral promise.
- Schwartz only wrote 7 paragraphs himself — he let the *author* (the Chinese medicine practitioner) speak in his own voice for the bulk of the ad. This is voice authenticity as proof.
- "Crude" layout is a feature, not a bug. Looks like an editorial article from a small health newsletter, not a sales pitch.
- Long bullet lists of conditions the technique allegedly addresses (heart disease, obesity, etc.)
- Survives 20 years against competitor rewrites — Schwartz says competitors had "strong, strong, strong wonderful copy" but couldn't beat this ugly, raw, mechanism-led ad.
