## Standout pattern
The "qualified disqualification" line — "(If you're under $500,000 in annual revenue this is not for you)" — does double duty. It's a scarcity device, a status signal ("we're for the bigger players"), and a self-selecting filter that makes the reader who IS over $500K feel chosen. The move worth stealing: build one explicit disqualifier into the headline area. It boosts perceived value, filters bad leads, and triggers reactance-bias in the right reader ("oh I qualify? then this IS for me").
