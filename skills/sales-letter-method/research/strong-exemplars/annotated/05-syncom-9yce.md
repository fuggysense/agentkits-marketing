## Standout pattern
The "good loan vs bad loan" reframe is the unique move. Instead of selling "I'll get you funded," Joe sells "I'll protect you from bad capital." This converts a commodity (loan brokerage) into an advisory relationship. The agitation is INVERTED — rather than "you can't get a loan," it's "you might get the WRONG loan, and that's worse." The move worth stealing: when your category is commoditized (loans, ads, leads), don't promise the commodity — promise the discrimination between good and bad versions of the commodity.
