## Standout pattern
The "list-everything-we-buy" approach is the entire move. Eleven property types listed before any positioning. It's not "we buy distressed homes" — it's "single unit, multi family, commercial, warehouses, parking spaces, empty lots, vacant, worn down, trailers, unwanted, and practically any piece of real estate." This converts a niche service into a yes-or-no qualification: if you have ANY of these and want out, the answer is "talk to us." The move worth stealing: when your offer is broad/transactional, lead with an exhaustive list of what qualifies. The reader scans for their own situation and self-qualifies.

## Failures / weaknesses (be honest)
