**Schauer's "Key Takeaways" (verbatim):**

> Are you filtering out & segmenting leads via your opt-in form. Think of what field you can include that will help you better understand & segment your list. Often times, a more detailed call-to-action is most effective. In this case they went from "Subscribe" to "Scroll down to subscribe" to prime visitors to scroll. Are you being too general in your CTA? Four reports has a higher perceived value than one. The visuals also make them look more valuable. How can you increase the perceived value of your lead-gen offer? The content of your lead magnets are crucial. How can you provide massive value & information while also further selling yourself such as with successful case studies.

## Weaknesses
- **Landing pages are not sales letters.** Most landing-page swipes on swiped.co rely on social proof + free trial + form, not on building belief through narrative. Pattern transfers only to top-of-funnel free-offer pages, not to high-ticket persuasion.

## Cross-niche transferability
- **Agency → agency:** direct transfer. Consulting sales letters from Kern/Deiss/Abraham are the closest documented template for Jerel's agency vertical.
- **Agency → info-products:** transfers because most info products are sold by ex-agency owners who teach what they used to do.
- **Agency → B2B SaaS:** partial. Qualification + risk reversal patterns transfer; but "I'll do it for you" framing has to become "the platform does it for you" — different proof requirements.
