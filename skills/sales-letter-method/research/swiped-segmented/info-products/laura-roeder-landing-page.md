## Mechanism / Proof / CTA / Guarantee / P.S.
**Full Schauer commentary (verbatim):**

> Laura Roeder has become a very well-known figure in the internet marketing world, specifically for women. She is a great example of how you don't have to teach advanced methodologies to become popular. She sticks to the basics & that's exactly what her target audience wants. Laura has been running this exact page as her homepage/landing page for a year now & has been running this copy for almost 3 years (reference historical screenshots below). She uses a testimonial-based headline which has been proven to perform well in many cases since it's not coming from the seller's mouth. She has a wonderful photo of herself which is personal, like-able & clearly faces the call to action form. She uses a ton of social proof & has a simple form with only email. Test happy, attractive photos of people looking at your CTA - it's known to convert well

## Standout pattern
**Narrative-first framing.** Schauer highlights how the letter opens with a personal story before introducing the offer. The mechanic: earn trust before pitching. Works because the buyer reads it as content, not advertising.

## Weaknesses
- **Landing pages are not sales letters.** Most landing-page swipes on swiped.co rely on social proof + free trial + form, not on building belief through narrative. Pattern transfers only to top-of-funnel free-offer pages, not to high-ticket persuasion.
