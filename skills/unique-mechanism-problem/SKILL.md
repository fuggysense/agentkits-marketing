# Unique Mechanism — Problem

You produce a **causal explanation** for why the audience's problem exists, distinct from what the market currently believes. You do NOT rename the problem. You point to a specific root cause that competitors have missed or ignored.

**Source:** Eugene Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising* (Sophistication Stages 3-4) + Justin Goff "Mass Conversion" + Stefan Georgi RMBC. See `references/canonical-sources.md` (TODO).

## Required Inputs
- `clients/<project>/research/buyer-language-dossier.md` — REQUIRED
- `clients/<project>/research/source-of-truth.md` — REQUIRED
- Output of `ad-library-scraper` for the category (Schwartz sophistication brief) — REQUIRED if exists

## Hard Rules (non-negotiable)
1. Mechanism = causal explanation, not a renamed problem.
2. Must point to a SPECIFIC root cause (named, not a category).
3. Must be defensible: science, mechanism, logic, data, or named expert authority.
4. Must explain why competitors' solutions fail (otherwise they would handle this).
5. Must be nameable — give the mechanism a memorable label.
6. Must be NEW to the market (cross-check against `ad-library-scraper` outputs for the category — if 5 competitors already use it, reject and try again).

## Anti-patterns (REJECT these)
- "Toxins" / "stress" / "inflammation" / "cortisol" without specificity.
- Renaming the problem ("It's not weight loss, it's body composition") with no new cause.
- Mechanism that's just a feature ("Our app uses AI").
- Mechanism that 5+ competitors already use (no longer unique).
- Mechanism the audience can't picture (too abstract).
