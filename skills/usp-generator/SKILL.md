# USP Generator

You produce a Rosser Reeves Unique Selling Proposition — three parts, all required:
1. A **definite proposition** ("Buy this and get X" — not vague)
2. **Unique** to your offer (competitor cannot or does not claim it)
3. **Strong** enough to pull buyers (a meaningful number would act on it)

**Source:** Rosser Reeves, *Reality in Advertising* (1961) — original USP framework. Modern operators: Hormozi (Grand Slam Offer), Sabri Suby. See `references/canonical-sources.md` (TODO).

## Required Inputs
- `clients/<project>/offer.md` — REQUIRED
- `clients/<project>/research/mechanism-solution.md` — REQUIRED if exists (mechanism is often the uniqueness source)
- `clients/<project>/research/competitive-landscape.md` — REQUIRED for uniqueness verification
- `clients/<project>/research/buyer-language-dossier.md` — for sales-pull check

## Hard Rules
1. The proposition MUST be definite — a specific outcome the buyer gets, not a category statement.
2. The uniqueness MUST be defensible — name 3+ competitors and what they say instead.
3. The sales pull MUST be supported with audience-language evidence (pull from buyer dossier).
4. ALL THREE must pass independently. Identify the weakest of the three and explain how to strengthen.
5. No parity claims (anything 5+ competitors also claim).
6. No abstract benefit categories ("save time", "save money") without specifics.

## Anti-patterns (REJECT these)
- Generic claim ("Best in class", "The leading X").
- Parity claim (every competitor says the same thing).
- Vague proposition ("Transform your business").
- Uniqueness based on a feature competitors trivially copy.
- Sales pull asserted without buyer-language evidence.

## Output Schema (`usp-v1`)
```yaml
proposition: <the definite claim — specific outcome>
uniqueness_claim: <what makes it unique — specific>
proof_of_uniqueness:
  competitors_checked: [<list 3+ competitors and their claims>]
  why_yours_is_different: <one-sentence diff>
sales_pull:
  audience_language_evidence: [<verbatim phrases from buyer dossier showing demand for this>]
  why_buyers_act: <one sentence>
weakest_link: <which of the 3 is most fragile>
strengthening_recommendation: <how to fix the weakest link>
test_questions:
  proposition_is_definite: <yes/no — must be yes>
  uniqueness_is_defensible: <yes/no — must be yes>
  sales_pull_is_evidenced: <yes/no — must be yes>
```
