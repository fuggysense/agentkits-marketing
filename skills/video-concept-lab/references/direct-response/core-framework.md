# Core DR Framework — Video Concept Stage

**Purpose:** the spine every paid-video concept must respect, regardless of format or duration. Read this BEFORE generating concepts. Apply as a structural filter, not as verbal copy to embed.

**Sources:** Schwartz (Breakthrough Advertising), Whitman (Cashvertising / LF8), Joyner (Irresistible Offer / Mental Movies / TIMER). Corrected and short-form-adapted by Iman Gadzhi take 2026-05-18 (see `iman-take-260518.md`).

---

## The non-negotiable spine

Every concept must reduce to:

> **pain → gap → mechanism → belief shift**

If a concept can't be reduced to those four moves, it is not a concept. It is content. Reject it.

---

## Layer 1 — Market Sophistication (FIRST decision)

Before any hook, angle, or visual: determine **how many similar claims the audience has already heard**. Strategy flows from this:

| Sophistication stage | Audience state | Concept strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1** | First to make any claim in this market | State the claim plainly. "Lose 10 lbs in 30 days." |
| **Stage 2** | Competitors making same claim | One-up it. Bigger, faster, cheaper. |
| **Stage 3** | Audience tired of escalation | Introduce a **mechanism**. "Lose 10 lbs because of mucosa-absorption iron." |
| **Stage 4** | Mechanism-based claims everywhere | New mechanism + new context / origin / story. |
| **Stage 5** | Cynical, jaded, seen it all | Identification + experiential proof + radical specificity. |

**Most wellness / B2B / DTC markets are Stage 3-5.** Plain claims fail. Mechanism is the bedrock.

---

## Layer 2 — Mental Movies via PVAs (delivery discipline)

Per Whitman: the script's job is to **install an Internal Representation (IR)** in the prospect's brain — not to describe the product.

Use **Powerful Visual Adjectives** across three sensory modes:
- **Visual:** "deep-dish bing cherry pie" not "pie"
- **Auditory:** "the crunch of the buttery crust giving way" not "crispy crust"
- **Kinesthetic:** "sweet cherry juice flowing out every time your fork sinks in" not "tastes great"

**Application:** every concept's hook + body beats must specify the sensory anchor, not the abstraction. The seeder MUST reject any concept whose pain or promise is stated in generic terms.

❌ "You feel tired"
✅ "You're dragging through your 2 PM meeting wondering why coffee stopped working"

---

## Layer 3 — Hell Island / Heaven Island (the Gap)

Per Joyner: tension to act comes from the **distance between two states**:

- **Hell Island:** the prospect's current pain reality — sensory, present-tense, "this second"
- **Heaven Island:** the future vision the prospect wants — also sensory, also concrete
- **The Gap:** the cost of staying on Hell Island, framed via TIMER (next layer)

**Short-form rule:** one clean pain image + one clean desired state. Don't try to paint the full island. Pick the one frame that crystallizes each.

Avoid "future pain" / prevention framing — people are motivated by what hurts NOW, not what might hurt later.

---

## Layer 4 — TIMER as Lens (not as copy)

Per Joyner — **T**ime / **I**dentity / **M**oney / **E**nergy / **R**eputation.

**Iman correction:** TIMER is the LENS behind the angle decision. It does NOT appear in verbal copy. The seeder uses TIMER to determine WHICH cost-of-staying-the-same to dramatize visually, not to list 5 costs in narration.

For each concept:
1. Pick the ONE TIMER dimension that hurts the buyer most
2. Make that dimension visible in the body (visual stakes, not narrated)
3. The other 4 TIMER dimensions remain unspoken but in the seeder's reasoning

Most ads will not state price. The "cost" being framed is the cost of NOT solving — visualized via the one TIMER dimension that lands hardest.

---

## Layer 5 — LF8 as Substrate (not as language)

Per Whitman: the **Life-Force 8** biological desires are the bedrock motivators:

1. Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension
2. Enjoyment of food and beverages
3. Freedom from fear, pain, and danger
4. Sexual companionship
5. Comfortable living conditions
6. To be superior, winning, "keeping up"
7. Care and protection of loved ones
8. Social approval

**Iman correction (load-bearing):** LF8 is the emotional **substrate**. It is NOT the verbal language. Forcing primal survival language ("don't die alone!") reads corny in B2B, wellness, and any sophisticated market.

**The discipline:** identify which LF8 desire(s) actually drive the buy, then translate to the market's NATIVE dialect. See `lf8-market-translation.md` for the per-market translation table.

**Test:** if your hook sounds like a 1950s pulp magazine, you over-rotated to literal LF8 language. Translate.

---

## Layer 6 — Body Structure (collapsed by duration)

The full Joyner/Whitman body (5 steps: point-pain → gap → plan/mechanism → belief via gradualization/mechanization → harpoon objections) is **VSL territory**. In short-form, collapse:

| Duration | Body structure |
|---|---|
| **15s** | hook + ONE pain image + ONE mechanism cue. That's it. |
| **30-60s** | hook + pain + gap + mechanism + ONE objection touch |
| **60s+ (VSL territory)** | full 5-step body acceptable |

**Iman warning:** "If you try to do present pain, future pain, mechanism, proof, objections, and CTA all with equal weight, the ad becomes mush." Short-form punishes completeness.

## Delivery Format Adaptations

Format is the WRAPPER, not the spine. Same DR spine across all formats. Specific delivery rules:

### Music / sung ads
- Sung hook = better hook vehicle. Same persuasion system.
- Best practice: melody carries the HOOK + emotional carrier; switch to spoken word for mechanism + proof
- **Don't sing everything** — discipline collapses when the song tries to BE the ad
- LF8 + open loops translate STRONGER in music (rhythm delivers desire faster than exposition)
- Where music fails: low-sophistication markets where the lyric never names the problem clearly; trust-heavy markets where music lowers perceived seriousness
- See `iman-take-260518.md` "Singing Ads Layer" section for the full Iman ruling

### UGC / talking-head
- Highest risk of dilution: creator slips into testimonial-storytelling instead of hook→pain→gap→mechanism
- Discipline: the creator's lines must map to the same DR beats as a scripted ad
- Use when the audience needs human-face belief (trust-heavy / identity-driven offers)

### Cartoon / 2D animation
- Highest risk: overproduction buries the mechanism in style
- Discipline: the visual metaphor must validate (or BE) the mechanism — not decorate around it

### Motion design / VFX
- Use when the mechanism benefits from visual transformation / process clarity
- Risk: abstraction loses the buyer; mechanism must be physically legible in 2 seconds

### Talking-head (founder direct-to-camera)
- Use when offer is trust-heavy + identity-driven (human face carries belief)
- Discipline: founder must hit DR beats, not narrate origin story

## Iman's three corrections to orthodox DR (mandatory)

Above and beyond the Schwartz/Whitman/Joyner orthodoxy, Iman flagged three pillars the framework systematically misses:

1. **Open-loop architecture** — each beat must carry an unanswered question into the next beat. The cardinal sin: 3 clips that all resolve simultaneously = zero tension, zero forward motion. Open loop = withheld answer, not a narrative flourish. See `concept-stage-mandatory-checks.md` for the per-beat check.

2. **Proof placement discipline** — proof must do ONE of three jobs: (a) validate the mechanism, (b) validate the specificity of the problem, (c) validate the identity of the buyer. Anything else is decorative. The seeder MUST tag every proof element with which of the three jobs it does.

3. **Compression discipline (the "intellectually complete" trap)** — short-form REWARDS precision, compression, and selective omission. It PUNISHES completeness. The best concepts make the right person feel the next sentence before you say it. The seeder must aggressively cut "complete" answers in favor of charged ones.

---

## Concept-vs-content test (final gate)

Before emitting any concept, the seeder must answer YES to all four:

1. Does it reduce cleanly to pain → gap → mechanism → belief shift?
2. Does it specify ONE TIMER dimension as the visual stakes (not narrate all 5)?
3. Does it translate LF8 to the market's native dialect (not use primal language)?
4. Does it pass the duration-collapse rule for the target length?

If any answer is no, it is not a concept. It is content. Reject.
