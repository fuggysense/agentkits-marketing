# DR Foundation — Theory + Operational Checks (merged)

**Purpose:** the complete DR methodology for paid-video concept generation. Theory + operational checklist combined into one file. Load this when the brief is **direct-response / paid cold conversion** (NOT for brand/awareness — see `../general/brand-awareness-methodology.md` for that path).

**Sources:** Schwartz (Breakthrough Advertising), Whitman (Cashvertising / LF8), Joyner (Irresistible Offer / Mental Movies / TIMER). Short-form adapted by Iman Gadzhi take 2026-05-18 (see `iman-take-260518.md` for verbatim transcript).

---

## The minimum-viable directive (embed in every dispatch)

> **"Know the level of awareness, hit one sharp pain, widen the gap, sell the mechanism, and only then choose the format that expresses it best."**

If the seeder violates this sentence, the concept is invalid. No exceptions.

---

## The non-negotiable spine

Every concept must reduce to:

> **pain → gap → mechanism → belief shift**

If a concept can't be reduced to those four moves, it is not a concept. It is content. Reject it.

---

## Mandatory pre-write order — 6 modules (NEVER fuse, NEVER reorder)

Generate concepts as discrete modules in this exact order. Do NOT produce a "fully fused creative blob" — that's where format invents strategy.

| # | Module | What gets decided | Reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Market Sophistication** | Which of Schwartz's 5 stages the audience sits at | Layer 1 below |
| 2 | **Angle** | The ONE belief / pain / desire this concept targets | brief / operator |
| 3 | **Promise** | What the prospect gets — translated through market's LF8 dialect | `lf8-market-translation.md` |
| 4 | **Proof type** | Person / process / metaphor — and which of 3 jobs | Pillar 2 below |
| 5 | **Format recommendation** | Chosen LAST via the 4-gate rubric (NOT inherited from upstream brief) | Format-Last section below |
| 6 | **Claim guardrails** | Allowed / forbidden expressions per brief | brief |

**Critical:** **Format is module 5, not module 1.** If upstream brief locks `workflow_flow`, TREAT IT AS A HYPOTHESIS not a constraint. Override if 4-gate rubric points elsewhere — flag override at AG0.

---

## Layer 1 — Market Sophistication (FIRST decision)

Before any hook, angle, or visual: determine **how many similar claims the audience has already heard**. Strategy flows from this:

| Stage | Audience state | Concept strategy |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | First to make any claim in this market | State the claim plainly |
| **2** | Competitors making same claim | One-up it (bigger / faster / cheaper) |
| **3** | Audience tired of escalation | Introduce a **mechanism** |
| **4** | Mechanism-based claims everywhere | New mechanism + new context / origin / story |
| **5** | Cynical, jaded, seen it all | Identification + experiential proof + radical specificity |

**Most wellness / B2B / DTC markets are Stage 3-5.** Plain claims fail. Mechanism is the bedrock.

---

## Layer 2 — Mental Movies via PVAs

The script's job: **install an Internal Representation (IR)** in the prospect's brain — not describe the product.

Use **Powerful Visual Adjectives** across three sensory modes:
- **Visual:** "deep-dish bing cherry pie" not "pie"
- **Auditory:** "the crunch of the buttery crust giving way" not "crispy crust"
- **Kinesthetic:** "sweet cherry juice flowing out every time your fork sinks in" not "tastes great"

**Reject** any concept whose pain or promise is stated in generic terms.

❌ "You feel tired"
✅ "You're dragging through your 2 PM meeting wondering why coffee stopped working"

---

## Layer 3 — Hell Island / Heaven Island (the Gap)

Tension to act comes from the **distance between two states**:

- **Hell Island:** prospect's current pain reality — sensory, present-tense, "this second"
- **Heaven Island:** future vision they want — also sensory, also concrete
- **The Gap:** cost of staying on Hell Island, framed via TIMER

**Short-form rule:** one clean pain image + one clean desired state. Don't paint the full island — pick the one frame that crystallizes each.

Avoid "future pain" / prevention framing — people are motivated by what hurts NOW.

---

## Layer 4 — TIMER as Lens (not as copy)

**T**ime / **I**dentity / **M**oney / **E**nergy / **R**eputation.

**TIMER is the LENS behind the angle decision. It does NOT appear in verbal copy.** Use TIMER to determine WHICH cost-of-staying-the-same to dramatize visually, not to list 5 costs in narration.

For each concept:
1. Pick the ONE TIMER dimension that hurts the buyer most
2. Make that dimension visible in the body (visual stakes, not narrated)
3. The other 4 TIMER dimensions remain unspoken but in the seeder's reasoning

---

## Layer 5 — LF8 as Substrate (not as language)

The **Life-Force 8** biological desires:

1. Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension
2. Enjoyment of food and beverages
3. Freedom from fear, pain, and danger
4. Sexual companionship
5. Comfortable living conditions
6. To be superior, winning, "keeping up"
7. Care and protection of loved ones
8. Social approval

**LF8 is the emotional SUBSTRATE. It is NOT the verbal language.** Forcing primal survival language ("don't die alone!") reads corny in B2B, wellness, and any sophisticated market.

**The discipline:** identify which LF8 desire(s) actually drive the buy, then translate to the market's NATIVE dialect.

→ **See `lf8-market-translation.md` for the per-market translation table (7 markets covered).**

**Test:** if your hook sounds like a 1950s pulp magazine, you over-rotated to literal LF8 language.

---

## Layer 6 — Body Structure (collapsed by duration)

Match body structure to target duration. Do not import VSL completeness into short-form.

| Target duration | Body structure (mandatory) |
|---|---|
| **15 seconds** | hook + ONE pain image + ONE mechanism cue. Nothing else. |
| **30-60 seconds** | hook + pain + gap + mechanism + ONE objection touch |
| **60s+ (VSL)** | full Joyner 5-step body acceptable (point-pain → gap → plan/mechanism → belief via gradualization/mechanization → harpoon objections) |

**Test:** count persuasion beats. If a 15s concept has 5 beats, it's a VSL squeezed into 15s — reject and collapse.

## The Three Iman Pillars (mandatory pre-emit checks)

Above and beyond orthodox Schwartz/Whitman/Joyner. Iman flagged these as the biggest gaps the orthodoxy misses for short-form video.

### Pillar 1 — Open-loop architecture

**Definition:** each beat must carry an unanswered question into the next beat. The loop is a WITHHELD ANSWER, not a narrative device.

**Cardinal sin:** 3 clips that all resolve simultaneously = zero tension, zero forward motion, just information.

**Per-beat check (every multi-clip concept):**
- Clip 1: what question does this OPEN that the viewer needs answered?
- Clip 2: does this CARRY the tension (gap widens, mechanism still unnamed) or RESOLVE it prematurely?
- Clip 3 (or final): does this CLOSE the loop from clip 1? Is the mystery answered, the mechanism named?

**Iman's annotated 15s example (wellness, Problem-Aware):**
- Clip 1: *"If you wake up tired no matter how much you sleep, this is usually not a motivation problem."* — LOOP OPENS ("if it's not motivation, what is it?")
- Clip 2: *"Most women keep treating it like a willpower issue, but the real cost is creeping into your focus, mood, and patience by 2 p.m."* — TENSION CARRIES (mechanism still unnamed)
- Clip 3: *"That's why we built a simple energy reset protocol that helps your body recover before the crash starts."* — LOOP CLOSES (mechanism named)

### Pillar 2 — Proof placement discipline

Every proof element must do ONE of three jobs. If it does none → decorative → strip.

| Job | What it does | Example |
|---|---|---|
| (a) **Validate mechanism** | Shows WHY the new mechanism works | "Mucosa-absorption bypasses the stomach lining" |
| (b) **Validate problem specificity** | Confirms buyer's pain is real and distinct | "73% of women with normal ferritin still report fatigue" |
| (c) **Validate buyer identity** | Confirms buyer is the right person for this | "I'm a 38yo mom of 2 who tried iron pills for 6 months" |

**Per-concept check:** tag every proof element with which of (a)(b)(c) it serves. Untagged = decorative = strip.

### Pillar 3 — Compression discipline (anti-intellectually-complete)

Short-form REWARDS precision, compression, and selective omission. It PUNISHES completeness.

**Per-concept check:**
- Could I cut 30% of body beats and still land the punch? If yes, cut.
- Does the concept "tell the whole truth" or set up the next sentence?
- Is every beat charged (forward motion) or complete (closed)?

**Iman's warning:** *"The biggest gap is that it still sounds a little too intellectually complete. That's dangerous."*

## Hook Archetype Decision

Two viable hook archetypes. Decision variable = **AWARENESS STAGE**, not format.

| Archetype | When to use | When to AVOID |
|---|---|---|
| **Problem-aware** | Audience already feels the pain. Default for cold traffic. | Never wrong, but feels generic to saturated/sophisticated audiences |
| **Mechanism-first** | Audience is colder/saturated/educated. Already feels pain. Needs a NEW LENS. | Fails on audiences who haven't felt the pain strongly enough — sounds vague, abstract, fake science |

**Examples (fatigue / women's wellness):**
- **Problem-aware:** *"Still crashing at 2 p.m. even after sleeping eight hours? That's because most women are chasing energy the wrong way."*
- **Mechanism-first:** *"The reason this works is because it targets the recovery layer your body is ignoring, not just the symptom you feel by afternoon."*

Mechanism-first is NOT a 2026 replacement for problem-aware. It is a sophistication-tier upgrade applied selectively. **Default to problem-aware for cold; upgrade to mechanism-first when audience is Stage 3-5 AND already pain-aware.**

Hook archetype is awareness-dependent first, NOT format-dependent first. UGC and cartoon can both carry mechanism-first if the mechanism is grounded and visually legible.

## Format-Last 4-Gate Rubric

Format is module 5 — chosen LAST. NOT a deterministic lookup table. Heuristic with hard constraints.

### Practical selection rules

| Angle character | Recommended format |
|---|---|
| Emotionally immediate + easily dramatized + creator-led | UGC or founder piece-to-camera |
| Depends on visual transformation, process clarity, or mechanism benefiting from motion | cartoon-flow or motion-design |
| Novelty, curiosity, or pattern interruption | viral preset clones |
| Trust-heavy + identity-driven | talking-head (human face carries belief) |

### The 4 gate questions (mandatory)

Before locking format, answer all 4:

1. **Can this be understood in 2 seconds?** (If no, format is wrong.)
2. **Does the proof look stronger as a person, a process, or a visual metaphor?**
3. **Does the creator have enough credibility to hold the format?**
4. **Will the format amplify the mechanism, or bury it?**

**Failure mode:** if 1+2 are yes but 4 is no → format is wrong, even if it "feels right."

### Multi-format output

If multiple formats pass all 4 gates, output the **top 2 with reasons**. Operator picks only when there's a genuine tradeoff. Single winner = no operator decision needed.

### Verdict on upstream format-locking

> Useful for production, dangerous for strategy. Locks creative bias before the real problem is diagnosed. Format is a rendering choice, not the starting assumption.

If `concept-brief.json` declared a `workflow_flow` upstream and the 4-gate rubric points elsewhere:
1. Flag the conflict at AG0 in the chat-only compass
2. Emit concepts in BOTH the declared format AND the 4-gate-recommended format
3. Let the operator choose at AG1

## Delivery Format Adaptations

Format is the WRAPPER, not the spine. Same DR spine across all formats.

### Music / sung ads
→ **See `singing-ads-layer.md` for the full sung-ad ruleset.**
Short version: sung hook = better hook vehicle. Don't sing everything. Melody carries hook + emotion; spoken word carries mechanism.

### UGC / talking-head
Highest risk of dilution — creator slips into testimonial-storytelling instead of hook→pain→gap→mechanism. Discipline: creator's lines must map to DR beats. Use when audience needs human-face belief.

### Cartoon / 2D animation
Highest risk: overproduction buries mechanism in style. Discipline: visual metaphor must validate (or BE) the mechanism — not decorate around it.

### Motion design / VFX
Use when mechanism benefits from visual transformation / process clarity. Risk: abstraction loses the buyer. Mechanism must be physically legible in 2 seconds.

### Talking-head (founder direct-to-camera)
Use when offer is trust-heavy + identity-driven. Discipline: founder must hit DR beats, not narrate origin story.

## Concept-vs-Content Test (final gate before emit)

Run all 6 BEFORE writing the concept JSON:

1. ✅ Does it reduce cleanly to **pain → gap → mechanism → belief shift**?
2. ✅ Does it follow the **6-module order** (sophistication → angle → promise → proof → format → guardrails)?
3. ✅ Does it pass the **duration-collapse rule** for target length?
4. ✅ Does it have a **clear open loop** that carries beat-to-beat?
5. ✅ Does every proof element have a **tagged job** (mechanism / problem specificity / buyer identity)?
6. ✅ Does the hook match the **awareness stage** (problem-aware default; mechanism-first only when warm)?

Any NO = not a concept. Reject. Regenerate.
