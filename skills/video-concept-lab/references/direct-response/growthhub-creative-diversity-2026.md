# GROWTHHUB.AGENCY — Winning Creative Strategy 2026

> Source: transcribed video deck. $40M tested in last 6 months of 2025. Meta-rewarded ("GEM & Andromeda") creative diversity framework.

## The 5 Creative Pillars

True creative diversification (not tweaks) across 5 axes:

1. **Persona Targeted** — Which smaller segment of your customers is being targeted?
2. **Angle** — What specific belief, pain, or desire is this speaking to?
3. **Awareness Level** — What level of product or problem awareness is this written for?
4. **Format Used** — What format delivers this message best for this persona and angle?
5. **Actors/Creators** — Who represents this persona authentically in tone, look, and delivery?

### Entity ID concept

If a creative has the same entity ID as a previous ad, Meta treats them as the same entity. Same learnings, same delivery. Small tweaks ≠ true creative diversification. To reach NEW audience cohorts, you need genuinely different entities across the 5 pillars.

## Pillar 1 — Personas

- Winning brands use **3–7 micro personas per product**.
- Example (supplements): Athletes / stressed professionals / older adults.
- **One persona = ceiling.**

### Personas are NOT demographics

Biggest mistake brands make.

**A persona is defined by:**
- Motivation
- Pain
- Desired outcome
- Lifestyle
- Buying triggers

**NOT by:** age, gender, income.

> A 25-year-old and a 45-year-old can have the same persona if their pain is identical.

### Persona Matrix (9 columns to fill per concept)

| Pain Point | Motivation | Agitate | Benefit | Desired Outcome | Discredit Alternatives | Objection | Social Proof | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Example row (BLUETEC 430):
| Buying a car is stressful | Mechanics charge for unnecessary repairs | Hidden issues cost thousands | Identifies issues before purchase, saving money | Peace of mind, no hidden fees | Relying on seller's word / basic visuals | "Can I really trust this tool?" | 500+ ratings, 4.8★ | Tested by mechanics and car experts |

## Pillar 2 — Angles

- Most brands use 1–2 angles. **Top brands test 10+.**
- Angles reflect core motivations: TIME, ENERGY, CONFIDENCE, IDENTITY, STATUS, RELIEF, CONSISTENCY, SIMPLICITY.

### Same angle, different "worlds"

**Angle: Save Time**
- Busy Mom — survive evenings
- Gym Bro — avoid meal prep
- Professional — reduce mental load

**More angles → more behavioral signals → faster algorithmic learning → more scale.**

## Pillar 3 — Awareness Levels

**80% of cold traffic is Unaware or Problem-Aware.**

| Stage | Definition | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Unaware | Aren't aware of need or desire | Educate / entertain |
| Problem-Aware | Know problem, not solutions | Stress pain / benefits |
| Solution-Aware | Know solution exists, not specific products | Prove your product is the solution |
| Product-Aware | Know your product, not convinced | Reinforce benefits |
| Most Aware | Know product and what it does | Introduce an offer |

### Ryze Coffee example

**Aware ad (Product-Aware):** "Here's why mushroom coffee helps you survive 2PM crashes."

**Scale script (Unaware/Problem-Aware):**
- A: "Man, after lunch my productivity crashes."
- B: "That's normal, caused by your breakfast."
- A: "What do my scrambled eggs have to do with this?"

Reframes beliefs and educates BEFORE selling.

## Pillar 4 — Format

Persona = Who | Angle = Why | Awareness = When | **Format = How**

### Ad duration strategy

- **Standard ads:** start selling at the beginning of the video.
- **Stealth creatives:** Hook → Educate → Entertain → **Sell later in duration.**
- Stealth creatives **do not look like ads initially.**

## How to use this framework for auditing a concept pack

For each concept, fill the 5-pillar grid:

| Concept | Persona | Angle | Awareness | Format | Actor/Creator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| c01 | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| c02 | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

**Pack-level diversity test:**
- Persona axis: Do concepts hit 3+ distinct micro-personas?
- Angle axis: Do concepts hit 3+ distinct angles?
- Awareness axis: Do concepts cover Unaware → Most Aware spread, OR concentrate on 80% (Unaware + Problem-Aware)?
- Format axis: Single format or multiple?
- Actor axis: Same face or multiple?

**If a pack is 5 concepts on the SAME persona × angle × awareness × format × actor cell = single Entity ID → fails creative diversification by GROWTHUB's standard.**

For each concept, fill the Persona Matrix (9 columns). Gaps = where the concept is weak.

Classify structure: Standard (sell early) or Stealth (sell late).
