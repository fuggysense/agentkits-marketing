# LF8 Market Translation Table

**Purpose:** translate Whitman's Life-Force 8 (the biological substrate) into each market's native dialect — the actual words the prospect would say to a friend.

**Why this exists:** Iman's correction to orthodox DR (see `core-framework.md` Layer 5). Forcing primal LF8 language ("survival!" / "social approval!") reads corny in modern markets. The seeder must translate substrate → dialect before writing any hook.

**Source:** Iman Gadzhi take 2026-05-18, F3 follow-up (see `iman-take-260518.md` lines 103-115).

---

## How to use this table

For every concept the seeder generates:

1. Identify the **micro-persona's market archetype** (from the 7 below — fall back to closest match if not listed)
2. Read the **core LF8 drives** for that archetype (1-2 of the 8 — the rest are noise)
3. Use the **native phrasing** column as the dialect lane for hook + body beats
4. **Test:** does the hook sound like something the prospect would say to a friend over coffee? If it sounds like a 1950s pulp magazine, you over-rotated to literal LF8 language

## The 7-market dialect table

| Market | Core LF8 Drives | Native phrasing (what they'd say to a friend) |
|---|---|---|
| **Wellness / women's health** | Freedom from pain, comfort, protection of loved ones | "I want to feel normal again." • "I want my energy back." • "I want to stop dragging through the day." • "I want to show up for my kids without feeling wrecked." |
| **B2B SaaS** | Freedom from pain, status, superiority, protection of team | "I want my team off my back." • "I want to look like we have our act together." • "I want a cleaner pipeline / faster closes / fewer fires." |

## Iman's caveat (mandatory)

> Sometimes dialect IS just the angle. LF8 is the underlying fuel; dialect is the surface expression. If the translation sounds generic, you haven't translated — you've just renamed the same abstraction.

**Discipline:** if the seeder produces "I want to feel better" for a wellness concept, that's not a translation — that's evasion. The dialect must be specific enough to feel like one buyer's actual words, not an abstraction layer.

---

## Non-obvious findings (worth remembering)

Iman flagged these because they're counter-intuitive — most marketers default to the wrong drive for these markets:

- **B2B SaaS is NOT about productivity.** It's about CONTROL and reduction of CHAOS. "I want a cleaner pipeline" beats "I want to be more productive" every time.
- **Financial is NOT just about security.** Status drives it harder than people admit. "Stop being behind everyone else" is a status play, not a security play.
- **Property agents buy reputation wrapped in income.** Don't sell leads — sell the look of being the go-to agent who happens to get leads.
- **Fitness is the most brutally primal.** "Look good naked" is the honest drive. Trying to spiritualize fitness ads ("become your best self") leaves money on the table.

## How to extend (when adding a new market archetype)

If a client's market doesn't fit the 7 above, the seeder should:

1. Identify the 1-2 LF8 drives that ACTUALLY drive the buy (not the ones the brand WISHES drove it)
2. Pull 4-6 verbatim phrases from `_swipe/winning-ads/*.marketing.md` for that market — those are the dialect proof
3. If no swipes exist, pull from `_brand/buyer-profile.md#micro-persona-map` "what they say to a friend" field
4. Add the new market row to this file under a `## Extensions` section so it carries forward
