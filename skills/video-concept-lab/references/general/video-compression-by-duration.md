# Video Compression by Duration — Beat Sheets for 15s / 30s / 45s / 60s / 90s

> **Why this file exists:** PAS lives in `framework-arsenal.md` (25/40/35 ratio). Transformation lives in `six-emotional-states.md` + `legend-architecture.md`. Both are copy-frameworks. Neither has per-duration video beat-sheets. The seeder and pack-builder need to pick the right structure for whatever `duration_target_seconds` the brief declares. This file is that lookup.

> **Authority:** `concept-brief.json.duration_target_seconds` is the source of truth. Legacy `concept-input-packet.json` is an alias only. No default. Operator declares it per brief based on rung-lift budget (see `_brand/funnel.md` if present) + platform conventions + concept needs.

---

## Master lookup table

| Duration | Word budget | Beats max | Rung-lift max | Best for | Worst for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **15s** | ~37 words | 3 | 0.5 rung (identity match) | Already at SL opening rung — confirms recognition. Reels/TikTok native. | Stage-4 discrediting (no room for moves). Mechanism reveals. |
| **30s** | ~75 words | 7-9 | 1 rung | 1 enemy + 1 reframe + simple mechanism. Meta DR sweet spot for problem-aware → solution-aware lifts. | 2+ enemies. 2+ reframes. Full Stage-4 unfolding. |
| **45s** | ~110 words | 9-12 | 1-2 rungs | 1 enemy + 1 reframe + mechanism with breathing room. Underused middle ground. | Long unfolding narratives. |
| **60s** | ~150 words | 12-15 | 2 rungs (tight) | 2 enemies, 1 reframe, full mechanism reveal + proof. Stage-4 discrediting. Solution-Aware × Stage-3 default. | Cold awareness-building (use 90s+). Brand spots. |
| **90s** | ~225 words | 15-20 | 3 rungs (still tight) | Unaware → Solution-Aware lift. Full Stage-4 with proof. Long-form DR. | Reels/TikTok native (drops fast). |

**Hard rule:** if concept needs 2+ rungs of lift, 2 reframes, or 2 enemies → minimum 60s. Don't force into 30s; the moves get muted.

## 15-Second Beat Sheets (identity-match only)

### PAS-15s
| Time | Function | Word budget |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5s | Hook (Problem OR Catalyst — not both) | ~12 |
| 5-12s | Body (one reframe OR one transformation specific) | ~17 |
| 12-15s | CTA | ~8 |

### Transformation-15s
| Time | Function |
|---|---|
| 0-5s | Before-world flash |
| 5-12s | After-world specifics |
| 12-15s | CTA |

**Reality:** 15s confirms recognition. Buyer must already be at the SL's opening rung. If you need them to LEARN something, pick 30s+.

## 30-Second Beat Sheets

### PAS-30s
| Beat | Time | Function | Word budget | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem hook | 0-5s | Name pain in buyer's verbatim language | ~12 | No setup. Open in the pain. |
| Agitate | 5-15s | Pain stack (3-4 specifics) OR Validation Flattery | ~25 | Solution-Aware × Stage-3: pair with `stage-4-discrediting.md` Move 2. |
| Solve + Mechanism | 15-25s | Big Idea / reframe + 1-2 specificity hooks | ~25 | Pair with `unique-mechanism-*` skill output. |
| CTA | 25-30s | Single action + soft handoff | ~10 | One verb. No qualifiers. |

### Transformation-30s
| Beat | Time | State (from six-emotional-states.md) | Word budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before-world | 0-5s | State 2 (Problem Awareness) | ~12 |
| Catalyst Moment | 5-10s | State 3 (Solution Awareness) | ~12 |
| Transformation unfold | 10-22s | State 4 (Product Awareness) | ~30 |
| Belief-shift | 22-26s | State 5 (Desire) | ~10 |
| CTA | 26-30s | — | ~8 |

**Catalyst Moment rule:** ONE sentence. Over-explain it and the arc collapses.

## 60-Second Beat Sheets (Stage-4 default)

### PAS-60s (Solution-Aware × Stage-3)
| Beat | Time | Function | Word budget | stage-4-discrediting move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Problem hook + validation | 0-8s | Name pain + "you didn't fail" line | ~20 | Move 2 (Validation Flattery) |
| Pain stacking | 8-22s | 4-5 hyper-specific symptoms | ~35 | Move 4 (Pain Stacking) |
| Anger recruit + enemy reveal | 22-32s | Name the false solution / institution | ~25 | Move 1 (Anger Recruitment) |
| Mechanism-as-foreclose | 32-48s | Big Idea + reframe + how it works | ~40 | Move 3 (Mechanism-as-Foreclose) |
| Product + proof + CTA | 48-60s | Answer + credibility stack + action | ~30 | — |

**Why 60s is the Stage-4 default:** all 4 discrediting moves get breathing room. 30s mutes Move 1 and Move 3 by 60-70%.

### Transformation-60s
| Beat | Time | Word budget |
|---|---|---|
| Before-world (extended, with multiple failed attempts) | 0-12s | ~30 |
| Catalyst Moment | 12-18s | ~15 |
| Transformation unfold (specifics, ≥4 concrete results) | 18-42s | ~60 |
| Belief-shift + named enemy | 42-52s | ~30 |
| CTA | 52-60s | ~15 |

## 90-Second Beat Sheets (Unaware-lifting cold traffic)

### PAS-90s
| Beat | Time | Function | Word budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook + setup | 0-10s | Pain + context | ~25 |
| Pain stacking + agitate | 10-30s | Hyper-specific symptoms + validation | ~50 |
| Failed prior attempts | 30-45s | Coat of Arms (what they've tried) | ~35 |
| Mechanism reveal + named enemy | 45-65s | Full Big Idea + enemy + how it works | ~50 |
| Proof + transformation | 65-80s | Credibility stack + specifics | ~40 |
| CTA + risk reversal | 80-90s | Action + guarantee | ~25 |

**Use case:** Unaware buyers in cold paid traffic. The ad does the full lift from no-awareness to ready-to-click. Most expensive per second; only viable when CAC math supports it.

## Combination Patterns (cross-duration)

### PAS-then-Transformation
PAS owns the first half, Transformation owns the second. Works at 30s, 45s, 60s. Don't try at 15s.

### Transformation-with-embedded-PAS
Single-protagonist arc IS the ad. Works at 60s+. Risk: pacing collapses below 60s.

### When to pick which
- **Multiple POV characters / lab-numbers-as-cheerleaders / chorus chants** → PAS lets you cut between scenes
- **Single-protagonist transformation journey** → Transformation natural fit
- **Singing ads** (per `singing-ads-layer.md`) — sung beats fit Transformation; spoken bridge fits PAS Mechanism reveal

---

## Failure Modes (per duration)

| Duration | Failure mode |
|---|---|
| 15s | Tries to lift more than 0.5 rung. Tries to do PAS + Transformation. Two CTAs. |
| 30s | Forces 2 enemies. Mechanism explanation >12s. CTA before second 24. |
| 45s | Lazy version of 60s — beats spread thin, no payload density. |
| 60s | Stage-4 moves blurred together (anger + validation + mechanism in one beat). Single proof type instead of stack. |
| 90s | Loses pacing in middle 30-45s. Buyer drops at second 50. |
