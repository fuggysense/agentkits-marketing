# Relatability Hook Examples

Mass-audience mirror. "That's me / that could be me."

## Operator-Supplied Examples

- "Why does nobody talk about the cost of staying in the wrong circle?"
- "I'm 34 and I'm afraid I've wasted too much time."
- "Have you ever felt completely under-prepared for an exam?"

## Why These Work

| Example | Relatability mechanism |
|---|---|
| Cost of the wrong circle | Universal fear — everyone has stayed in a situation too long |
| I'm 34 and afraid | Age-transcendent. The fear of wasted time is universal across age groups. |
| Under-prepared for an exam | Mass-audience shared experience — almost everyone has felt this |

## Notes on the Emotion Bridge

The viewer's specific situation doesn't have to match the hook exactly. Tagging the underlying emotion is the universal bridge.

- A 22-year-old watches a 34-year-old say "I'm afraid I've wasted too much time" → relates via the fear, not the age.
- A non-student watches "under-prepared for an exam" → relates to the feeling of being unprepared, not the exam itself.

Strong relatability hooks tap emotions: loneliness, isolation, ambition, fear of wasting time, cost of the wrong circle, imposter syndrome, not being taken seriously. These are universal. Use them.
