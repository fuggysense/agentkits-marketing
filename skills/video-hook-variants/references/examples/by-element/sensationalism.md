# Sensationalism Hook Examples

Huge contrast or extreme. Pattern interrupt. Wow factor.

## Operator-Supplied Examples

- "Our parents just gave us $600k to open a gaming lounge."
- "This is how I sold my company for $10 million in three days."
- "Is it possible to ace a midterm with just one hour of studying?"
- "400 slices of bread… can you actually get drunk off it?"

## Why These Work

| Example | Sensationalism mechanism |
|---|---|
| $600k from parents | Huge number + parents as source (unexpected) + young entrepreneurs |
| Sold company for $10M in 3 days | Extreme outcome + extreme speed |
| Ace midterm in 1 hour | Extreme efficiency — violates normal expectations |
| 400 slices of bread | Physical absurdity — visual impossibility |

## Claim Risk Warning

**Sensationalism under high claim_risk is the #1 source of forbidden_expression violations.**

Before using sensationalism:
1. Validate every claim against `allowed_expressions` in concept-brief.json.
2. If claim_risk = high → treat Sensationalism as guarded. Default to Relatability + Stakes.
3. A weaker hook beats a flagged one. Claim safety wins — always.

When claim_risk is not flagged, sensationalism is the fastest path to a high-scroll-stop rate. Use it aggressively where claims are verified.
