# Text Hook Examples

On-screen text overlay. 3–5 bold words appearing within 0:00–0:02. NOT subtitles.

## Operator-Supplied Examples

- "POV: your mom's live reaction to you making $150,000/month at 19"
- "I'm 34 and I'm afraid I've wasted too much time"
- "Our parents just gave us $600,000 to open a gaming lounge"
- "This is how I sold my company for $10 million in three days"

## Why These Work

| Example | Hook mechanism |
|---|---|
| POV: mom's live reaction… | Relatability (family dynamic) + Sensationalism ($150k/month) + Stakes (emotional moment) |
| I'm 34 and afraid… | Relatability (fear of wasted time is universal) + Stakes (identity threat) |
| $600,000 from parents | Sensationalism (huge sum) + Stakes (young people, parental trust on the line) |
| Sold company for $10M | Sensationalism (extreme outcome) + curiosity gap (how?) |

## Notes for Drafting

- Text pre-loads topic context before the VO catches up — people read faster than they hear.
- Text is NOT subtitles. It carries its own message that reinforces (but does not repeat) the visual and verbal.
- Readable in a single glance: high contrast font, minimal words.
- Combined with visual, text sets the stage so the verbal can pivot immediately into the hook's payoff rather than having to establish context.
