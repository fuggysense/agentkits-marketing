# Visual Hook Examples

What the eye sees first. Works with sound off.

## Operator-Supplied Examples

- 400 slices of bread stacked wall-to-wall in front of the camera
- A massive pile of cash (220k+) dropped on a table
- A Gen-Z kid standing in a cluttered old light shop surrounded by dusty lamps
- Two uni students posing in an empty warehouse with "$600k from parents" text flashing

## Why These Work

| Example | Hook mechanism |
|---|---|
| 400 slices of bread | Absurdity — the visual is so extreme that a plain VO works. Scroll-stopper via physical impossibility |
| Cash pile on table | Sensationalism (huge amount) + immediate topic signal (money) |
| Gen-Z in old light shop | Contrast (young person in old-world setting) + stakes signal (saving the family business) |
| Empty warehouse + text | Sensationalism ($600k) + stakes (two young entrepreneurs risking it all) |

## Notes for Drafting

- High motion, high color, or high contrast preferred — but a perfectly composed static shot can work if the subject is extreme enough.
- The visual must communicate topic OR emotional context before any audio loads.
- If the visual is the dominant layer, the VO can be simpler — the visual carries the hook. See `references/frameworks/lego-bricks.md` (Visual-Supplement-Verbal trick).
