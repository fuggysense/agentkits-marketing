# Nuclear Hooks — All Three Elements

Hooks that hit Relatability + Sensationalism + Stakes simultaneously. These usually explode.

## Operator-Supplied Examples

**"POV: your mom's live reaction to you making $150k a month at 19"**
- Relatability: family dynamic, parental approval — universal emotional territory
- Sensationalism: $150k/month at 19 — extreme outcome
- Stakes: the emotional stakes of a parent's live reaction to their child's success

---

**"Our parents just gave us $600k to open a gaming lounge. Will we build Melbourne's best one… or burn through their money before we turn 23?"**
- Relatability: young entrepreneurs, family trust, fear of failure
- Sensationalism: $600k from parents, gaming lounge
- Stakes: "burn through their money before we turn 23" — financial loss + identity + hard age deadline

---

**"I'm 34 and I'm afraid I've wasted too much time — here's how I brute-forced my way into Harvard design school and still felt lost."**
- Relatability: fear of wasted time (universal, age-transcendent)
- Sensationalism: Harvard + brute-forced (implies non-traditional path)
- Stakes: "still felt lost" — achieving the goal wasn't enough (subverts expectations, creates deeper curiosity)

---

**"My mom's cozy little light shop in Singapore will fail if I (a clueless uni student) don't save it."**
- Relatability: helping parents, Gen-Z stepping up for family
- Sensationalism: Gen-Z student vs. Boomer business owner dynamic
- Stakes: family business collapse + "30 years my mom built"

---

## Pattern Analysis

Nuclear hooks share three structural traits:

1. **Specific, concrete setup** — not "my parent's business" but "my mom's cozy little light shop in Singapore." Specificity makes sensationalism believable.
2. **Unresolved tension** — the hook opens a loop the viewer must close. "Will we build Melbourne's best one… or burn through their money?" is unanswered. The viewer must stay to find out.
3. **Emotional anchor** — at least one element triggers an emotional response (pride, fear, hope, shock). Emotion drives the opt-in decision.

## Usage Note

Nuclear hooks require all three elements to be genuine — not forced. If Sensationalism requires a claim not in `allowed_expressions`, downgrade to Strong (2 elements) rather than fabricating or exaggerating.
