# Recommended Sitemap: SG TCM + Physiotherapy Clinic Website

**Research Basis:** Analysis of 6 established Singapore TCM clinics (PULSE TCM, Oriental Remedies Clinic, Ma Kuang TCM, Yong Kang TCM, Yi TCM, Better Wellness TCM)

**Target:** Single-location clinic with 2–3 physicians, integrated TCM + physiotherapy services

---

## Executive Summary

Singapore TCM clinics follow a **consistent 7–9 page structure**. This sitemap is grounded in real, active clinic navigation patterns and embeds SG-specific conventions (WhatsApp booking, bilingual EN/中文, MOH credentials visibility, CHAS/BG panel info) that clinic managers expect to see.

---

## Core Sitemap (7 Pages)

### 1. **Home**
- Hero section with clinic name, value prop (e.g., "Evidence-Based TCM + Physiotherapy for Fast Recovery")
- Short intro: specialty focus (pain mgmt, fertility, sports injury recovery, etc.)
- Highlight core services in 3–4 visual cards (Acupuncture, Physiotherapy, Herbal Medicine, Consultation)
- Key social proof: patient count, years in operation, MOH registration badge
- Primary CTA: "Book Appointment" (button, WhatsApp link, phone)
- Quick clinic info: address, hours, phone, distance from MRT

### 2. **About Us**
- Clinic story: founding year, mission, evolution into TCM + physiotherapy integration
- Why choose us: 3–5 unique value propositions
- Clinic facilities: treatment rooms, equipment (modern vs. traditional balance)
- Credentials: MOH registration number, healthcare group affiliations if any
- Team photo/intro (brief; full bios on "Our Practitioners" page)

### 3. **Services** (Primary navigation hub)
- Grid/list of 5–8 core treatments:
  - **Acupuncture & Moxibustion** → sub-page with technique explanation, conditions helped, time per session
  - **Herbal Medicine (Customized Remedies)** → sub-page on consultation process, herbal sourcing
  - **Tuina Massage (Chinese Therapeutic Massage)** → sub-page on pressure points, duration, pain levels
  - **Cupping & Gua Sha (Scraping)** → sub-page with safety info, marks expected, benefits
  - **Physiotherapy (Western + TCM Integration)** → sub-page on rehabilitation protocols, sports recovery
  - **Consultation & Diagnosis** → sub-page on traditional diagnosis methods (pulse, tongue, questioning)
  - **(Optional) Wellness Programs** → e.g., corporate wellness, seasonal detox packages

Each sub-page includes:
- Detailed description of technique
- Conditions it treats (linked to "Conditions We Treat" page)
- Typical session duration & frequency
- Safety info & FAQ (acupuncture needle size, pain level, bruising, contraindications)
- CTA to book

### 4. **Conditions We Treat**
- List of 25–35 conditions, organized by category:
  - **Pain & Musculoskeletal:** Back pain, neck stiffness, shoulder tension, knee pain, sports injury, arthritis, sciatica
  - **Women's Health:** Fertility, irregular periods, menopause, gynecological concerns
  - **Stress & Sleep:** Anxiety, insomnia, stress-related illness, headaches/migraines
  - **Digestive & Metabolic:** Bloating, constipation, weak digestion, weight management
  - **Immune & Seasonal:** Low immunity, allergies, asthma, common cold recovery
  - **Pediatric:** Child's cough, digestive issues, concentration/focus (if applicable)

Each condition:
- 1–2 sentence explanation of how TCM addresses it
- Recommended treatments (e.g., "Back pain → Acupuncture + Tuina Massage + Herbal Medicine")
- Link to relevant service page

### 5. **Our Practitioners**
- Dedicated bio for each physician/therapist:
  - **Name** (English + Chinese if applicable)
  - **MOH Physician License Number** (mandatory for credibility in SG)
  - **Qualifications:** TCM degree, additional certifications (acupuncture, sports medicine, fertility, etc.)
  - **Years of Experience**
  - **Specialties & Treatment Focus**
  - **Languages:** English, Mandarin, (other if applicable)
  - **High-quality headshot**
  - **Short bio:** 2–3 sentences on philosophy, patient success stories, or expertise highlights

### 6. **Locations & Hours**
- Clinic address (full, with postal code for Google Maps)
- **Google Map embed** with clinic marker
- Operating hours: clearly listed by day (e.g., "Mon–Fri 11am–8pm, Sat 10am–6pm, Closed Sun")
- Parking info: nearby carpark, whether clinic offers validation, distance from MRT/bus stop
- Contact phone number & WhatsApp link
- Email address for inquiries

### 7. **Book Appointment**
- **Multi-channel booking prominently displayed:**
  - Online calendar widget (Calendly, Acuity Scheduling, or clinic's booking system)
  - WhatsApp direct link (primary in SG context)
  - Phone number with clickable call-to-action
- Brief form: patient name, date/time preference, service type, first-time visitor checkbox
- Note: "Walk-ins welcome but appointments recommended"
- FAQ: "What to bring on first visit?" / "Cancellation policy" / "Late cancellations"

## Optional Extensions (Pages 8–9)

### 8. **Blog / Health Resources**
- 5–10 articles on health topics:
  - "How Acupuncture Works: A Beginner's Guide"
  - "Back Pain Myths Debunked"
  - "TCM + Physiotherapy: Why Integration Works Faster"
  - Seasonal wellness tips (e.g., "Spring Detox with TCM")
  - "Ask the Physician" Q&A posts
- Each article: 800–1500 words, includes call-to-action to book related service
- **SEO benefit:** long-tail keywords (e.g., "best acupuncture for back pain Singapore")

### 9. **Contact & FAQ**
- Contact form (name, email, phone, message, service inquiry dropdown)
- FAQ section addressing common questions:
  - "Do acupuncture needles hurt?" / "Is acupuncture safe?"
  - "How many sessions until I see results?"
  - "Do you accept health insurance / CHAS / BG panels?"
  - "What's the cost of a typical session?"
  - "How should I prepare for my first appointment?"
  - "Do you treat [specific condition]?"
- Social media links (Facebook, Instagram typically)
- Privacy policy & terms of service links

## SG-Specific Web Conventions (Non-Negotiable)

| Convention | Why | Implementation |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp Booking** | Preferred SG booking channel; 95% of clinics use it | WhatsApp button on every page (top nav, footer, booking page); direct link with pre-filled message |
| **Bilingual EN/中文** | Clinics serve English + Mandarin-speaking patients | Language toggle (top-right header) or auto-detect; all critical pages in both languages |
| **MOH Physician License Display** | Legal requirement for credibility; patients verify online | Every physician bio includes MOH Reg. No.; link to MOH Physician Search (hpp.moh.gov.sg) if possible |
| **CHAS/BG/IP Panel Badges** | Signals affordability & insurance acceptance | Homepage footer or About page: "Accepted: CHAS / Pioneer Panel / IP Panels / AIA / NTUC / etc." |
| **Google Maps Embed** | Local trust signal; enables directions | "Locations" page includes interactive map; review section visible |
| **Operating Hours (Prominent)** | SG clinics operate late; patients book around working hours | Display on homepage footer AND Locations page; include public holiday schedule |
| **WhatsApp Clinic Name** | SG convention for clinic contact | WhatsApp messages arrive with clinic name pre-filled in patient's phone |

## Next Steps

1. **Adapt specialist focus:** If clinic emphasizes pain management (like Better Wellness), feature that on homepage & "Conditions" page. If fertility-focused (like Yi TCM), lead with that specialty.
2. **Fill in clinic-specific content:** Bios, story, conditions, hours, location details.
3. **Design & UX:** Use the footer navigation and mobile-first WhatsApp booking as design anchors.
4. **Launch blog gradually:** Start with 3–5 health articles; SEO payoff emerges after 6+ months of content.
5. **Verify MOH registration:** Ensure physician credentials & license numbers are accurate before publishing.
