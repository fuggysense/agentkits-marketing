RECOMMENDED 7-PAGE CORE STRUCTURE
================================================================================

1. HOME
   └─ Hero value prop + core services intro + CTA to book
      MOH badge, phone, WhatsApp, hours

2. ABOUT US
   └─ Clinic story, mission, team intro, MOH registration
      Why choose us (3-5 value props)

3. SERVICES
   ├─ Acupuncture & Moxibustion
   ├─ Herbal Medicine (Customized Remedies)
   ├─ Tuina Massage (Chinese Therapeutic Massage)
   ├─ Cupping & Gua Sha (Scraping)
   ├─ Physiotherapy (Western + TCM Integration)
   └─ Consultation & Diagnosis
      [Each sub-page: technique, conditions helped, duration, safety FAQ, CTA]

4. CONDITIONS WE TREAT
   ├─ Pain & Musculoskeletal (back, neck, shoulder, knee, arthritis, sciatica)
   ├─ Women's Health (fertility, periods, menopause)
   ├─ Stress & Sleep (anxiety, insomnia, headaches)
   ├─ Digestive & Metabolic (bloating, constipation, weight mgmt)
   ├─ Immune & Seasonal (allergies, asthma, cold recovery)
   └─ Pediatric (cough, digestion, focus)
      [25-35 conditions, linked to service pages]

5. OUR PRACTITIONERS
   └─ Physician bio cards:
      • Name (EN + 中文)
      • MOH License Number [MANDATORY]
      • Qualifications & certifications
      • Years of experience
      • Specialties
      • Languages
      • Headshot
      • Short bio

6. LOCATIONS & HOURS
   ├─ Full address (postal code for Google Maps)
   ├─ Interactive Google Map embed
   ├─ Operating hours (by day)
   ├─ Parking info & MRT distance
   ├─ Phone & WhatsApp link
   └─ Email

7. BOOK APPOINTMENT
   ├─ Online calendar (Calendly / Acuity Scheduling)
   ├─ WhatsApp direct link [PRIMARY SG BOOKING CHANNEL]
   ├─ Phone number (clickable)
   └─ FAQ: first visit prep, cancellation policy, late arrivals

OPTIONAL:
8. BLOG / HEALTH RESOURCES (5-10 SEO-friendly articles)
9. CONTACT & FAQ (generic inquiry form + common Q&A)

================================================================================
SG-SPECIFIC WEB CONVENTIONS (MANDATORY)
================================================================================

✓ WhatsApp Booking Button
  └─ On every page; primary booking channel in SG (95% of clinics use it)

✓ Bilingual EN/中文
  └─ Language toggle at header; all critical pages in both languages

✓ MOH Physician License Display
  └─ Every physician bio must include MOH Reg. No.; link to verification

✓ CHAS / BG / IP Panel Badges
  └─ Homepage/About: "Accepted: CHAS / Pioneer Panel / [insurance names]"

✓ Google Maps Embed
  └─ "Locations" page; enables directions, shows reviews

✓ Operating Hours (Prominent)
  └─ Header footer AND Locations page; 11am–8pm typical

✓ Phone Number (Clickable)
  └─ Mobile users expect tap-to-call; prominent on header/footer
